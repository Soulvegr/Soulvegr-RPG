3. # **SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION**

   1. ## **1.1 Welcome to the Advanced Epoch**

Congratulations—you've survived the Initiate Epoch. Your characters are no longer scrappy adventurers scavenging Divine Crystal shards or fleeing from low-level House operatives. **Welcome to the Advanced Epoch (Levels 11–20)**, where you become system-spanning legends who:

* Command fleets powered by **12-Crystal Matrices**   
* Negotiate with **Divine House Champions**   
* Wield Powers that reshape reality itself   
* Navigate the **soul economy** (understanding its temptations and dangers)   
* Encounter the **Bloom** firsthand and witness the Void's true nature   
* Begin crafting with **Divine Crystals** to create legendary equipment

✓ **10 new Advanced Powers per Channeling Origin** (120 total), each with the familiar 3-tier progression

4. # **SECTION 2: EPOCH TRANSITION**

Entering the Advanced Epoch transforms your character from capable adventurer to system-shaping legend. Section 2 covers the mechanical requirements, revised AP economy, and narrative milestones that mark this ascension.

1. ## **2.1 Requirements and Rituals**

   1. ### **Transition Trigger:**

**Level 11** (group reaches 20 Session Score points after Level 10\)

To unlock the Advanced Epoch, you must complete **ONE of these paths** (GM approves narrative fulfillment):

2. ### **Path 1: Mentor Bond**

**Complete a significant quest for a powerful mentor:**

**Living Demigod (Divine House Champion)**

* **Quest Example:** Defend a Divine Crystal mine from rival House assault, negotiate territorial treaty, assassinate House enemy   
* **Benefit:** Access to Realm-specific training, Divine House connections, Crystal mine privileges   
* **Cost:** Loyalty oath—mentor can demand service, assign missions, involve you in House politics 

**Echo Ancestor (Ghostly Remnant)**

* **Quest Example:** Recover lost Crystal fragment from Void-touched ruins, complete unfinished ancestral oath, purify corrupted family relic   
* **Benefit:** Hidden lore about Soul Cycle, bloodline-specific Powers, no living political ties   
* **Cost:** Haunted by ancestor's old grudges—ancient enemies may recognize your bloodline 

**Soul-Avatar (Crystallized Consciousness)**

* **Quest Example:** Bond with sentient Divine Crystal fragment, survive possession trial, prove worthy of merged consciousness   
* **Benefit:** Direct power boost, perfect Crystal affinity, instant mastery of specific Realm   
* **Cost:** Possession risk—Avatar may attempt to take control (Spirit DC 25 to resist) 

  3. ### **Path 2: Blood Awakening**

**Survive a transformative experience that reveals your Realm connection:**

**Near-Death Experience**

* Reduced to 0 HP by Realm-aligned damage (e.g., Void exposure, Divine House Champion's attack, Crystal backlash)   
* Your soul resonates with a specific Realm during death's threshold   
* **Roll d12 or GM assigns based on how you nearly died** (see Blood Awakening table below) 

**Divine House Encounter**

* Direct interaction with House founder or high-ranking champion   
* They recognize dormant Realm potential in your soul   
* May offer recruitment, test your worthiness, or attempt to eliminate you as threat 

**Void Exposure**

* Survive prolonged contact with Bloom Edge zones   
* Reality erosion reveals cosmic connections normally hidden   
* **Warning:** This path carries mutation risk (consult GM) 

  4. ### **Path 3: Soul Sacrifice**

**Perform a dangerous ritual using soul energy:**

**Requirements:**

* Sacrifice **50 Spark-grade souls** in a Vitastone (Life Crystal) ritual   
* Requires access to soul shards (legal contracts, black market, Tulip Freighter raid)   
* Takes 8 hours at a place of power 

**Risks:**

* **Echo Manifestation:** 2d6 Echoes appear within 1d4 days, hunting you   
* **Divine House Mortis Attention:** Soul burning on this scale attracts their enforcers   
* **Void Expansion:** Local Bloom advances 10 miles (reality destabilizes nearby) 

**Why Choose This Path:**

* No mentor obligations   
* No bloodline complications   
* Immediate, guaranteed advancement   
* **But at terrible cost**—you're starting Advanced Epoch with enemies 

  5. ### **Immediate Mechanical Rewards (All Paths):**

Upon completing your chosen path:

✓ **Stat cap rises to 25** (from 20\)  
✓ **Skill cap rises to Rank 20** (from 10\)  
✓ **\+20 one-time AP bonus**  
✓ **Access to Advanced Powers** (can now purchase Tier 1 Advanced Powers from any Channeling Origin)  
✓ **Access to Legend Talents** (powerful customization options)  
✓ **Soul grade increases** (Ember → Spark minimum, Spark → Flame if already Spark)

6. ### **Transition Ritual (Narrative Milestone)**

**No roll required—pure narrative moment.**

**Step 1: Choose a Place of Power**

Hold your ceremony at a location resonating with cosmic significance:

* **Divine Crystal mine** (channel Realm energy directly)   
* **Ancient battlefield scar** (where reality was reshaped by war)   
* **Derelict throne room** (abandoned seat of power)   
* **Soulvegr gate** (threshold between life and death)   
* **Bloom Edge outpost** (where reality meets entropy) 

**Step 2: Declare Your Legend's Purpose**

Speak aloud what you intend to become:

* *"I claim mastery of War, to forge strength through eternal conflict"*   
* *"I embrace Shadow, to guard secrets the universe must never know"*   
* *"I defy Death itself, to reshape the Soul Cycle"* 

**Your words matter.** Divine Houses may hear. Eternals may notice. The Void may respond.

**Step 3: GM Narrates Cosmic Acknowledgment**

The universe recognizes your ascension:

* **Star alignment** (constellations shift, forming Realm symbols)   
* **Divine House omen** (proxy arrives with offer or threat)   
* **Void tremor** (reality flickers, Bloom pulses, distant unmaking)   
* **Crystal resonance** (nearby Divine Crystals glow in response)   
* **Soul whispers** (Echoes manifest briefly, acknowledging your significance) 

**This is the moment heroes become legends.**

2. ## **2.2 New AP Economy**

Advanced Epoch progression accelerates to match legendary scope:

1. ### **AP Gains:**

* **AP per Level:** 12 AP (vs. 10 in Initiate Epoch)   
* **Session Scoring:** Unchanged (max 10 points per session, 20 total \= level up)   
* **One-Time Transition Bonus:** \+20 AP (at Level 11 only) 

  2. ### **Revised Spending Costs:**

| Category | Cost per \+1 |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Core Stats (21-25)** | **8 AP per point** |
| **Skills (11-15)** | **5 AP per rank** |
| **Skills (16-20)** | **10 AP per rank** |
| **Advanced Powers** | **See below** |
| **Legend Talent** | **20 AP** |
| **Advanced Perk** | **15 AP** |

     3. ### **Advanced Power Costs:**

**Each Channeling Origin offers 10 new Advanced Powers (120 total across all 12 Origins)**

| Tier | AP Cost | Prerequisites |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Tier 1** | **15 AP** | **Level 11+, Skill Rank 11+ in relevant skill** |
| **Tier 2** | **\+10 AP** | **Tier 1 of same power** |
| **Tier 3** | **\+15 AP** | **Tier 2 of same power** |

**Total cost for fully mastering one Advanced Power: 15 \+ 10 \+ 15 \= 40 AP**

4. ### **Fast-Track Options (Use with Caution):**

**1\. Soul Burning**

* **Burn 1 Flame-grade soul \= \+20 AP immediately**   
* **Consequences:**   
  * Echo haunt (2d6 Echoes manifest within 1d4 days)   
  * Divine House Mortis dispatches soul-hunters   
  * Local Bloom expansion (Void grows)   
  * Moral weight (your character gains power through cosmic imbalance) 

**2\. Mentor Training**

* **Double AP from 1 session** (if your mentor trains you)   
* **Cost:** Service owed (mentor assigns dangerous mission, demands loyalty, involves you in House politics)   
* **Benefit:** No soul burning, no Echoes, legitimate advancement 

**3\. Divine Crystal Purification**

* **Purify corrupted Divine Crystal shard \= \+2 AP**   
* **Requirements:** Crafting DC 25, Sanctuary or Life Crystal ritual   
* **Benefit:** Clean advancement, no enemies created   
* **Limitation:** Corrupted Crystals are rare 

  5. ### **AP Economy Example:**

**Kaine (Level 11 Warrior) receives transition bonus:**

* **\+20 AP** from transition   
* **Current total:** 20 AP to spend immediately 

**Spending options:**

* **Option 1:** Buy 1 Advanced Power Tier 1 (15 AP) \+ save 5 AP   
* **Option 2:** Buy 2 Core Stat increases to 21-22 (16 AP) \+ save 4 AP   
* **Option 3:** Buy 4 Skill ranks 11-15 (20 AP total) 

**Recommended:** Most players buy at least 1 Advanced Power Tier 1 to immediately feel more powerful.

5. # **SECTION 3: EXPANDED SKILLS**

Advanced Epoch characters push their skills far beyond mortal limits, reaching legendary proficiency that supports their new Powers and epic challenges. Section 3 covers the expanded skill caps and how higher ranks change gameplay.

1. ## **3.1 Higher Skill Caps**

**Advanced Epoch Skill Cap:** Ranks 11–20 (raised from Initiate cap of 10\)

1. ### **Simple AP Costs:**

| Rank Range | AP Cost per Rank |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Ranks 11–15** | **5 AP per rank** |
| **Ranks 16–20** | **10 AP per rank** |

**No additional costs, materials, or requirements—just pure skill investment through practice and experience.**

2. ### **What Higher Ranks Represent:**

**Ranks 11-13:** **Expert**  
You're among the best in your region. Starship pilots who can navigate dense asteroid fields, diplomats who negotiate with planetary governors, crafters who create masterwork equipment.

**Ranks 14-16:** **Master**  
You're among the best in your star system. Legendary fighters whose techniques are studied, hackers who break corporate encryption, negotiators who sway Divine House representatives.

**Ranks 17-19:** **Grandmaster**  
You're among the best in multiple systems. Your reputation precedes you. Divine Houses recruit you. Rivals fear you. Apprentices seek you.

**Rank 20:** **Legendary**  
You're one-in-a-galaxy. There might be a dozen people in the entire cosmos at your level. Your name is spoken with reverence. Ancient texts reference skills like yours. **You redefine what's possible.**

3. ### **Rank Benefits:**

| Rank | Capability | Example Challenge |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Rank 11+** | **Handle DC 25–30 challenges routinely** | **Navigate through Bloom-edge reality distortions** |
| **Rank 13+** | **Accomplish what others consider impossible** | **Craft with Divine Crystals without catastrophic failure** |
| **Rank 15** | **Among the best in a star system** | **Negotiate with Divine House Champions** |
| **Rank 17+** | **Mythic expertise, near-perfect execution** | **Pilot through collapsing Soulvegr gate** |
| **Rank 20** | **Legendary mastery (DC 35+ possible)** | **Create 12-Crystal Matrix, convince Divine House to change policy** |

   2. ## **3.2 Advanced Techniques**

High skill ranks unlock **natural techniques**—no extra bookkeeping, just better results:

1. ### **Rank 15+: Expertise Advantage**

**GMs grant Advantage OR \+5 to checks where your expertise clearly applies.**

**Examples:**

* **Melee Combat Rank 15+:** When fighting humanoid opponents with weapons you're familiar with   
* **Technology Rank 15+:** When hacking systems you've studied or encountered before   
* **Persuasion Rank 15+:** When negotiating with cultures you understand   
* **Crafting Rank 15+:** When working with materials you've mastered 

**GM Discretion:** If your expertise clearly gives you an edge, you get the bonus. This represents your deep understanding giving you tactical advantages others don't have.

2. ### **Rank 17+: Success with Complication**

**Once per scene, treat a failed check as a success with complication.**

You're so skilled that even your failures accomplish something—just with unexpected consequences.

**Examples:**

**Melee Combat Rank 17 \- Failed Attack Roll:**

* **Success:** You hit the enemy   
* **Complication:** Your weapon gets lodged in their armor, giving you Disadvantage on your next attack OR they grab your weapon, contested check to maintain grip 

**Technology Rank 17 \- Failed Hacking Check:**

* **Success:** You bypass the security   
* **Complication:** You trigger a silent alarm, guards will arrive in 2 rounds OR you leave a trace that can be tracked back to you later 

**Persuasion Rank 17 \- Failed Negotiation:**

* **Success:** They agree to your terms   
* **Complication:** They demand a significant favor in return OR word spreads that you had to work hard to convince them (damaged reputation) 

**Crafting Rank 17 \- Failed Divine Crystal Infusion:**

* **Success:** The item is created   
* **Complication:** It has a 15% malfunction chance OR it attracts unwanted attention from Divine Houses 

  3. ### **Rank 20: Legendary Expertise**

**Two benefits at Rank 20:**

**1\. Auto-Succeed on DC 25 or Lower**

In your specialty, you simply don't fail routine challenges anymore:

* **Melee Combat 20:** DC 25 combat maneuvers (disarms, trips, precision strikes) automatically succeed   
* **Technology 20:** DC 25 hacks automatically succeed   
* **Persuasion 20:** DC 25 negotiations automatically succeed   
* **Crafting 20:** DC 25 items automatically crafted without failure 

**2\. Add Narrative Flair to Successes**

When you succeed on checks, you can describe **how impressively you succeed**:

**Examples:**

**Melee Combat 20:**

* *"I don't just parry the attack—I redirect it into the enemy beside them, using their own ally's momentum against them."* 

**Technology 20:**

* *"I don't just hack the door—I rewrite their entire security protocol, making myself an authorized administrator for the rest of this facility."* 

**Persuasion 20:**

* *"I don't just convince them to help—I make them believe it was their idea, that supporting us serves their own interests perfectly."* 

**Crafting 20:**

* *"I don't just create the weapon—I forge it in a way that makes Divine House Artifex scouts take notice, they'll want to study my technique."* 

**GMs:** Let Rank 20 characters feel legendary. Their expertise should reshape situations in dramatic, campaign-affecting ways.

4. ### **Additional Rank 20 Examples by Skill:**

**Combat Skills:**

* **Melee Combat 20:** Crits automatically trip, disarm, or reposition foes (your choice)   
* **Ranged Combat 20:** Ignore half cover, shoot around corners, trick shots become routine 

**Social Skills:**

* **Persuasion 20:** Failed social check still shifts Attitude 1 step (even failures are impressive)   
* **Deception 20:** Can maintain multiple cover identities simultaneously without flaw   
* **Intimidation 20:** Mere reputation can scatter lower-threat opponents (below CR 5\) 

**Knowledge Skills:**

* **Technology 20:** Hacks auto-bypass security DC 25 or lower, can hack from remote location   
* **Mysticism 20:** Identify any Power or magical effect on sight, know its weaknesses   
* **Medicine 20:** Perform battlefield surgery without penalty, stabilize dying allies as Bonus Action 

**Crafting Skills:**

* **Crafting 20:** Create masterwork items in half time, add unique signature flourish   
* **Engineering 20:** Repair catastrophic ship damage mid-combat, jury-rig solutions that shouldn't work 

  3. ## **3.3 Skill Synergies**

High skills **amplify your Advanced Powers** without new subsystems.

1. ### **Power \+ Skill Synergy (Rank 15+ in Relevant Skill):**

When you use a Power and have **Rank 15+ in a supporting skill**, you gain benefits:

**Choose ONE per Power use:**

* **Advantage** on Power attack/save rolls   
* **\+2 damage/DC** on this use

5. ### **Why This System Works:**

✓ **Clean progression to cap 20** \- Simple AP costs, no complex subsystems  
✓ **Skills feel impactful without bloat** \- High ranks give clear, powerful benefits  
✓ **High ranks naturally enhance Advanced Powers** \- Synergies make specialization rewarding  
✓ **GMs can set ambitious DCs** \- Knowing players can reach Rank 20 means DC 30-35 challenges are fair  
✓ **Simple, effective, legendary** \- No complicated tables, just clear progression and dramatic capability

**Your skills are the foundation of your legend. Powers are flashy, but skills are reliable. Invest wisely.**

6. # **SECTION 4: ADVANCED POWERS SYSTEM**

   1. ## **4.1 Advanced Powers Overview**

Advanced Powers represent the pinnacle of each Channeling Origin's potential—legendary abilities that transform capable heroes into system-shaking forces of nature. Where Initiate Powers (Levels 1-10) gave you your foundation, Advanced Powers (Levels 11-20) define your legend.

1. ### **What Makes Advanced Powers Different:**

**Scale:** Initiate Powers affect individuals or small groups. Advanced Powers reshape battlefields, influence entire factions, and alter reality itself.

**Complexity:** Advanced Powers have more dramatic effects, longer durations, and greater consequences.

**Requirements:** You need Level 11+, higher skill ranks, and narrative milestones to learn them.

**Cosmic Significance:** Using Advanced Powers attracts attention—Divine Houses notice, mentors demand explanations, and the Void responds to reality-warping displays.

2. ### **Power Progression Reminder:**

Each power has **3 tiers** representing increasing mastery:

| Tier | Represents | AP Cost |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Tier 1** | **Basic mastery \- learning the power** | **15 AP** |
| **Tier 2** | **Intermediate mastery \- enhanced effects** | **\+10 AP** |
| **Tier 3** | **Complete mastery \- legendary effects** | **\+15 AP** |

**Total to fully master one Advanced Power: 40 AP (15 \+ 10 \+ 15\)**

3. ### **How Many Powers Should You Have?**

**There is no "correct" number.** Your power selection should reflect:

* Your character's training and mentors   
* Your experiences and challenges   
* Your legend's narrative arc   
* Your playstyle preferences 

**Typical Advanced Epoch character (Level 11-20):**

* **Specialist:** 3-5 Advanced Powers from 1-2 Origins, all at Tier 2-3   
* **Generalist:** 8-12 Advanced Powers from 4-6 Origins, mix of Tier 1-2   
* **Master:** 2-3 Advanced Powers at Tier 3 (complete mastery of signature abilities)

3. ## **4.3 Power Mechanics**

   1. ### **Casting Powers:**

**Action Economy:** Most powers require **1 Action** unless specified otherwise (Bonus Action, Reaction, etc.)

**Casting Check:** Roll **d20 \+ Relevant Attribute \+ Skill Rank**

**Target Number:**

* **Contested:** vs. target's relevant defense (e.g., your Mind \+ Mysticism vs. their Spirit \+ Resistance)   
* **Set DC:** GM determines based on difficulty (DC 15-30 typical for Advanced Powers) 

**Save DC (when power forces a save):** **8 \+ Proficiency Bonus \+ Relevant Attribute Modifier**

2. ### **EP Costs:**

Advanced Powers cost more EP than Initiate Powers:

| Tier | Typical EP Cost |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Tier 1** | **4–6 EP** |
| **Tier 2** | **6–9 EP** |
| **Tier 3** | **9–13 EP** |

**Specific costs are listed with each power.**

3. ### **Range and Duration:**

**Range Categories:**

* **Self:** Affects only you   
* **Touch:** Must touch the target   
* **Short (30-60 ft):** Nearby targets   
* **Medium (90-120 ft):** Battlefield range   
* **Long (150+ ft):** Extended range 

**Duration Categories:**

* **Instant:** Effect happens immediately   
* **Rounds:** Lasts X combat rounds (6 seconds each)   
* **Minutes:** Short-term (1-10 minutes)   
* **Hours:** Extended (1-8 hours)   
* **Permanent:** Effect lasts until dispelled or broken 

  4. ### **Concentration:**

Some Advanced Powers require **Concentration** to maintain:

* You can only concentrate on **one power at a time**   
* Taking damage requires a **Vitality save** (DC 10 or half damage taken, whichever is higher)   
* Failing the save ends concentration   
* You can voluntarily end concentration at any time (no action required) 

Powers requiring Concentration are marked with **(C)** in their description.

4. ## **4.4 Purchasing Advanced Powers**

To purchase an Advanced Power, you must meet ALL of these requirements:

✓ **Character Level 11+** (Advanced Epoch)

**Example:** To learn *Eternal Oath* (Eternity Channeling), you need:

* Level 11+
