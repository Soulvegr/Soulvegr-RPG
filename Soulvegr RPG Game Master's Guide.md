3. # **SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION**

   1. ## **1.1 Welcome, Game Master**

Soulvegr challenges players to rise from Initiate Epoch mortals through Advanced heroes to Ascended demigods, but you reveal the hidden machinery driving their journey—the Twelve Eternals, Divine Crystals, the Soul Cycle, and the devouring Void. As GM, you narrate runeblade-starship clashes, embody scheming Divine Houses, and adjudicate the cosmic balance where the Godforge's creation battles the Void's unmaking.

This guide unveils the full scope withheld from the Player's Handbook: the truth about Divine Crystals and their role in legendary crafting, the complete Soul Cycle from death to reincarnation (and soul burning's terrible cost), the Void's reality-erasing horrors and mutation mechanics, and frameworks for wielding these truths across all three Epochs.

**What Players Know:**

* Souls are valuable currency   
* Divine Houses rule various sectors   
* Technology and magic coexist   
* Death can be cheated with SLDs   
* Divine Crystals are rare crafting materials 

**What You Know:**

* The Twelve Eternals created the 12 Realms of Influence as fundamental cosmic laws   
* Divine Houses are ancient organizations that unknowingly serve the Eternals   
* Divine Crystals are physical manifestations of Realm power and essential for certain high-level crafting   
* The Godforge can elevate mortals to cosmic significance—**but this is seen as a fundamental violation of cosmic order by nearly everyone in power**   
* The Void seeks to unmake everything the Godforge creates   
* **Souls are the backbone of the cosmic system**—they fuel advancement, power the Soul Cycle, and sustain the balance between Creation and Destruction   
* The Void mutates those who survive exposure to it   
* Reality itself is negotiable near the Bloom   
* **Mortals accessing the Godforge is the ultimate taboo—Divine Houses will do ANYTHING to prevent it**

  2. ## **1.2 The Soulvegr Cosmos Unveiled**

Players see swords-to-starships adventure; you wield the underlying cosmic truths.

1. ### **The Twelve Eternals**

The **Twelve Eternals** are primordial forces that predate even the oldest Divine Houses. They are not gods—they are fundamental aspects of reality itself, each governing one of the **12 Realms of Influence**:

1. **Eternity** \- Timelessness, endurance, unbreakable oaths   
2. **Truth** \- Revelation, honesty, knowledge laid bare   
3. **Shadow** \- Secrets, stealth, hidden paths   
4. **Creation** \- Matter, fabrication, bringing form to thought   
5. **Life** \- Vitality, healing, growth   
6. **Time** \- Causality, flow, inevitability   
7. **Sanctuary** \- Protection, refuge, sacred ground   
8. **War** \- Conflict, conquest, strength through struggle   
9. **Void** \- Entropy, nullification, unmaking   
10. **Death** \- Endings, harvest, necessary conclusion   
11. **Liberation** \- Freedom, breaking chains, revolution   
12. **Dominion** \- Control, command, hierarchy 

    2. ### **The Cosmic Balance**

The Eternals exist in **six paired opposites**, each pair maintaining equilibrium:

**Eternity ↔ Time**: The permanent vs. the flowing  
**Truth ↔ Shadow**: Revelation vs. concealment  
**Creation ↔ Void**: Making vs. unmaking  
**Life ↔ Death**: Beginning vs. ending  
**Sanctuary ↔ War**: Protection vs. conflict  
**Liberation ↔ Dominion**: Freedom vs. control

When these forces become unbalanced, reality itself suffers:

* Too much Creation without Void \= chaotic overgrowth, resource depletion   
* Too much Dominion without Liberation \= stagnant tyranny, no change   
* Too much Life without Death \= immortal crowding, ecosystem collapse   
* Too much Void without Creation \= total annihilation, the Bloom consumes all 

The Eternals enforce balance through their greatest creations: **the Godforge** (tool of Creation) and **the Void** (tool of Destruction).

3. ### **Core Cosmic Mysteries**

**The Godforge**

A mythical realm-engine capable of elevating mortal souls to cosmic significance. It is the ultimate tool of Creation, forging new realities, new gods, and maintaining the cosmic balance.

* **Location:** Unknown (theories include: galaxy core, distributed across 12 locations, pocket dimension, inside oldest Soulvegr gate)   
* **Access:** Requires mastery of all 12 Realms (collecting Throne Fragments, assembling Divine Crystal matrices, proving worthiness to the Eternals themselves)   
* **Purpose:** Allows worthy mortals to ascend and claim Realm authority 

**Why Divine Houses Fear It:**

The Godforge represents **existential threat** to the established order. If mortals access it, they can:

* Overthrow existing Divine House leadership   
* Redistribute Divine Crystal mine control   
* Rewrite Soul Cycle protocols   
* **Challenge the Eternals themselves** 

**This is not hyperbole.** A mortal claiming Eternal authority is viewed as:

* **Blasphemy** by Divine Houses   
* **Cosmic treason** by Pantheons   
* **Reality-threatening** by the Eternals themselves 

**What Happens When Mortals Seek the Godforge:**

Divine Houses will:

* Hunt you across the multiverse   
* Burn entire planets to stop you   
* Form temporary alliances with hated enemies   
* Deploy their most powerful champions   
* Sacrifice billions to prevent your success 

The Eternals will:

* Send cosmic-level threats (Voidborn horrors, reality collapses)   
* Test you mercilessly (most fail and are unmade)   
* Judge your worthiness (99.99% are found wanting)   
* **Potentially allow it IF you prove the current system has failed** 

**Can mortals actually replace Eternals?**

**Theoretically: Yes.** The Godforge was created for this purpose—to allow the cosmic order to evolve.

**Practically: Almost impossible.**

* Only a handful of mortals in all of history have even attempted it   
* Fewer still have survived the attempt   
* **None have succeeded in recorded history** (though legends speak of the "First Ascended" who may have become the current Eternals eons ago) 

**The Path to Godforge Success Requires:**

1. Mastery of all 12 Realms (opposing forces must be balanced)   
2. Collecting all 12 Throne Fragments (each guarded by impossible challenges)   
3. Assembling perfect 12-Crystal matrices   
4. Surviving the united opposition of EVERY Divine House   
5. Passing Eternal judgment (proving the current system must change)   
6. Actually being RIGHT that the cosmos needs you more than the current Eternals 

**If you succeed:**

* You ascend to Eternal-level authority   
* The cosmic order reshapes around your vision   
* You become the very thing you fought (power corrupts)   
* **You will face the same challenges from future mortals** 

**If you fail:**

* Best case: Death   
* Likely case: Soul destroyed (True Death, no resurrection)   
* Worst case: Retroactive unmade (you never existed)   
* Catastrophic case: Your failure destabilizes reality, accelerates the Bloom, and condemns entire civilizations 

**The Godforge is not a prize. It is a test. Most who seek it should fail.**

**The Void**

Not empty space, but **hyper-dense unmaking**—a realm where entropy collects. It is the ultimate tool of Destruction, consuming imbalance and resetting failures.

**The Void contains:**

* **Echo Remnants:** Burned souls denied proper Soul Cycle processing   
* **Unmade Matter:** Raw potential that failed to stabilize into reality   
* **Voidborn Entities:** Beings native to entropy (not invaders—they belong there)   
* **Corrupted Throne Shards:** Fragments of failed Godforge attempts   
* **Erased Histories:** Events and people retroactively unmade 

**The Void's Effects:**

* **Direct Contact (unprotected):** Atomic disintegration within rounds   
* **Proximity (Bloom Edge zones):** Mutations, reality instability, gear corruption   
* **Extended Exposure:** Memory loss, identity erosion, transformation into Voidborn 

**The 12 Divine Crystals**

Physical manifestations of each Realm of Influence. While rare legendary items can be crafted without them, Divine Crystals are:

1. **Essential for specific high-level crafting:**   
   * Reality Anchors (hold back the Bloom)   
   * Ley-Drive starship cores   
   * Godforge access keys   
   * Soul containment devices   
2. **Powerful optional amplifiers for legendary items:**   
   * Add Realm-specific properties to weapons/armor   
   * Enhance existing enchantments   
   * Grant thematic abilities tied to the Crystal's Realm

**Souls: The Backbone of Reality**

Souls are not merely currency—they are the **fundamental building blocks** of the cosmic system. Everything in Soulvegr connects back to souls, but not everything requires burning them.

**What Souls Power:**

**Through Proper Cycling (Safe, Sustainable):**

* Character advancement (earning AP through play, not consumption)   
* Divine House operations (processed souls fuel cosmic infrastructure)   
* Divine Crystal formation (souls naturally crystallize into Realm energy over eons)   
* Reality stability (Soul Cycle maintains balance)   
* Resurrection (through SLDs or Divine House services) 

**Through Soul Burning (Fast, Dangerous, Destabilizing):**

* Immediate Advancement Points (shortcut to power)   
* Emergency cosmic interventions (desperate measures)   
* Forbidden rituals and artifacts   
* **This creates imbalance—Echoes manifest, Void expands** 

**The Critical Distinction:**

**Players advance primarily through play (earning AP via Session Scoring), NOT soul burning.**

Soul burning is:

* A temptation (quick power NOW)   
* A moral choice (is it worth the cost?)   
* A dangerous shortcut (spawns Echoes, attracts Divine House attention)   
* **Not the intended path** (the Soul Cycle is meant to recycle, not burn) 

**Think of it this way:**

* **Soul Cycle \= renewable energy** (sustainable, balanced, slow)   
* **Soul Burning \= strip mining** (fast, powerful, destructive, causes collapse) 

The cosmos functions on the Soul Cycle. Soul burning is **corruption** of that system—it works, but it breaks things.

**Divine Houses**

Ancient organizations (not bloodlines, though some claim lineage) that embody the 12 Realms of Influence. They unknowingly serve the Eternals as instruments of cosmic balance.

* Each House controls access to their Realm's Divine Crystal mines   
* Houses form **Pantheons** (alliances of 12 Houses, one per Realm)   
* Pantheons enforce cosmic law and maintain regional balance   
* Imbalanced Pantheons (missing Realms) attract Void expansion 

**The 12 Divine Houses:**

* House Eternal (Eternity)   
* House Veritas (Truth)   
* House Umbra (Shadow)   
* House Artifex (Creation)   
* House Vitae (Life)   
* House Chronos (Time)   
* House Sanctuary (Sanctuary)   
* House Vortheon (War)   
* House Nullis (Void)   
* House Mortis (Death)   
* House Libertas (Liberation)   
* House Dominion (Dominion) 

**The Bloom (Entropy Wave)**

An expanding wave of Void corruption spreading from the galactic edge. This is what happens when cosmic balance fails:

**Causes:**

* Soul burning exceeds proper recycling   
* Divine Crystal mining outpaces natural reformation   
* Pantheon imbalance (missing or suppressed Realms)   
* Excessive immortality (SLDs preventing natural death cycle) 

**Effects:**

* Stars go dark (not exploding—vanishing)   
* Reality becomes thin and unstable   
* Physics breaks down   
* Voidborn entities manifest   
* Mutations occur in exposed populations   
* Territories become unmade (retroactive erasure) 

**The Bloom is not evil—it's the cosmic immune system responding to imbalance.**

3. ## **The Cosmic Order and Mortal Ascension**

**The Divine Houses' Perspective:**

For tens of thousands of years, Divine Houses have maintained cosmic order. They see themselves as:

* **Stewards of reality** (protecting the balance)   
* **Guardians against chaos** (preventing cosmic collapse)   
* **Rightful rulers** (they've earned their position through millennia of service) 

**Mortals accessing the Godforge threatens all of this.**

From a Divine House perspective, mortal Godforge-seekers are:

* **Delusional** (you think you can do better than us?)   
* **Dangerous** (your failure will kill billions)   
* **Blasphemous** (the Eternals chose US, not you)   
* **Existential threats** (if you succeed, we lose everything) 

**Even "good" Divine Houses (Sanctuary, Life, Liberation) will oppose you** because:

* They believe the current system, flawed as it is, is better than the chaos your attempt will cause   
* They've seen mortals try before (all failed catastrophically)   
* They fear you'll become corrupted by the power (absolute power corrupts absolutely) 

**The Eternals' Perspective:**

The Eternals are not "good" or "evil"—they are **functions**. They enforce cosmic law.

**They will allow mortal ascension IF:**

1. The current system has genuinely failed (proof required)   
2. The mortal can restore balance better than existing structures   
3. All 12 Realms are properly represented (no imbalance)   
4. The mortal survives their tests (most don't) 

**They will crush mortal ascension IF:**

* It's motivated by personal ambition (not cosmic need)   
* It would create imbalance (claiming only some Realms)   
* The mortal is unworthy (fails the tests)   
* It threatens the fundamental Cosmic Design 

**The Eternals don't care about mortal lives, Divine House politics, or individual suffering. They care about balance.**

If your ascension maintains balance: They might allow it.  
If your ascension threatens balance: They will unmake you without hesitation.

**The Hidden Truth:**

Some scholars theorize the **current Eternals were once mortals** who successfully claimed the Godforge eons ago. If true:

* The system CAN be overthrown   
* The Eternals know this and fear worthy challengers   
* Your success means they lose power   
* **They have every reason to make the tests impossible** 

But this is speculation. No one knows for certain.

**Your players will shape the cosmos—will they maintain the Eternals' design, or shatter it entirely?**

5. ## **GM Guidance: Running Godforge-Seeking Campaigns**

**If your players seek the Godforge, make it clear:**

1. **This is the hardest possible campaign path**   
   * Every major faction opposes you   
   * Failure is more likely than success   
   * The stakes are cosmic (billions of lives)   
2. **Opposition will be united and merciless**   
   * Divine Houses that normally hate each other will ally against you   
   * Assassins, armies, reality-warping champions   
   * They will kill your allies, destroy your homes, erase your histories   
3. **The Eternals are testing you, not helping you**   
   * Every challenge is designed to break you   
   * Passing means proving you're better than the current system   
   * Failing means you were never worthy   
4. **Success changes everything**   
   * You become the new cosmic order   
   * You face the same challenges the Eternals faced   
   * Future mortals will try to overthrow YOU   
5. **This is endgame content (Level 25-30)**   
   * Not appropriate for Initiate or early Advanced Epoch   
   * Requires mastery of all systems   
   * Player choice should be fully informed 

**Most campaigns should NOT involve Godforge attempts.** It's the ultimate endgame, reserved for tables that want to literally reshape reality.

**Alternative Endgame Options:**

* Join a Divine House (become part of the system)   
* Reform a Pantheon (change the system from within)   
* Defeat the Bloom (save reality without claiming godhood)   
* Found your own faction (carve out independence) 

**The Godforge path is available, but it should feel impossible, dangerous, and likely to end in tragedy.**

4. # **SECTION 2: PREPARING TO RUN SOULVEGR**

Soulvegr campaigns shine brightest when built on shared storytelling vision and a welcoming table. Section 2 focuses on creating that foundation through open discussion, player buy-in, and flexible campaign seeds that hint at the deeper cosmic truths—Divine Crystals, the Soul Cycle, and the Bloom's expansion.

1. ## **2.1 Session Zero and Group Contracts**

Session Zero anchors your campaign, typically 2-4 hours before characters take full shape. This investment pays dividends throughout your entire campaign by establishing expectations, boundaries, and shared excitement.

1. ### **Opening the Conversation**

Start with collaborative worldbuilding. Ask what excites players about Soulvegr:

**Genre Blending:** *"What sounds coolest—sword duels on starship decks? Mystic rituals powering hyperdrives? Necromancers negotiating with AI?"*

**Divine House Intrigue:** *"Do you want political manipulation, House vs. House conflicts, or serving a patron House?"*

**Divine Crystals:** *"Interest in crafting legendary weapons? Hunting rare materials? Understanding cosmic mysteries?"*

**Souls & Death:** *"Comfortable with SLD body-swapping? Curious about soul economics? Interested in resurrection mechanics?"*

**The Void:** *"Want cosmic horror elements? Reality-warping threats? Existential stakes? Mutations?"*

Let their answers guide your campaign focus. A table excited about crafting and exploration needs different prep than one craving House politics and betrayal.

2. ### **Safety Tools**

Soulvegr contains potentially intense themes: body horror (soul/body separation, mutations), existential dread (the Void, the Bloom), moral complexity (soul burning), and cosmic insignificance. Establish safety tools:

**X-Card:** Any player (including you) can tap a card or say "X-Card" to pause and skip/fade-to-black uncomfortable content. No explanation required. No questions asked.

**Lines and Veils:**

* **Lines:** Content we won't include (e.g., "No torture scenes," "No body horror," "No possession")   
* **Veils:** Content that happens off-screen (e.g., "Soul burning happens but we don't narrate the screaming," "Void unmaking is described vaguely," "Mutations happen between sessions") 

**Specific Soulvegr Discussions:**

* *"Void exposure can cause mutations and memory loss—are we okay with character transformation and forgetting NPCs/allies temporarily?"*   
* *"Soul burning creates moral dilemmas—comfortable with 'power at terrible cost' themes?"*   
* *"Body-swapping via Frames can be jarring—want to explore identity questions or keep it mechanical?"*   
* *"The Bloom is existential horror—okay with 'stars going dark' dread?"*   
* *"Mutations can be serious (body horror) or silly (extra fingers, backwards knees)—what's your comfort level?"* 

Document agreed boundaries in your group contract.

3. ### **The Group Contract**

Draft a simple agreement covering:

**1\. Practical Basics:**

* Session length: 3-4 hours recommended   
* Frequency: Weekly? Bi-weekly? Monthly?   
* Attendance: How many players needed to run? (Minimum 3 recommended)   
* Communication: How to notify if absent? (24 hours notice preferred)   
* Location: In-person, online, hybrid? 

**2\. Table Conduct:**

* Phones/distractions: At the table or away?   
* Food/drinks: Allowed? Who provides?   
* Spotlight sharing: Everyone gets moments to shine   
* Constructive conflict: PCs can disagree; players should collaborate 

**3\. Content Boundaries:**

* Safety tools in use (X-Card, Lines/Veils)   
* Tone preferences: Grimdark? Heroic? Morally gray? Mix of serious and silly?   
* Themes to include/avoid based on Lines/Veils discussion 

**4\. Cosmic Horror Handling:**

* *"If Void scenes become too intense, we'll check in with a simple thumbs up/down"*   
* *"Memory loss mechanics require GM notes; players trust the process"*   
* *"Soul burning consequences are serious—we'll telegraph before irreversible choices"*   
* *"Mutations will be rolled on tables—we can reroll if something crosses a line"* 

**5\. Character Death:**

* True Death is possible but rare (SLDs prevent it)   
* We'll discuss resurrection options if SLD fails   
* Replacement characters can join within 1-2 sessions   
* Voidborn transformation ≠ character death (still playable, just changed) 

  4. ### **Setting the Tone**

Align on starting power level and campaign flavor:

**Starting Epoch:** Almost always **Initiate Epoch (Levels 1-10)**. Characters are capable mortals, not gods. They've heard rumors of Divine Crystals but haven't seen them. They know Divine Houses exist but aren't power players yet.

**Campaign Flavor** (pick 1-2 primary themes):

* **Crystal Hunters:** Scavengers seeking Divine Crystals in ruins and war zones   
* **House Operatives:** Agents working for (or against) a Divine House   
* **Frontier Survivors:** Life on the edge of civilization where law is weak   
* **Soul Traders:** Navigating the morally complex soul economy   
* **Void Defenders:** Protecting reality from the Bloom's advance   
* **Revolution:** Liberation-focused characters fighting tyranny   
* **Explorers:** Discovering ancient secrets and cosmic mysteries 

**Starting Location:**

* The Spires (high-tech, House-controlled)   
* The Frontier (lawless, resource-scarce)   
* A specific planet or station you've prepared   
* Near the Bloom Edge (for Void-focused campaigns) 

  5. ### **The Shared Vow**

End Session Zero with a group affirmation. This sets the collaborative tone:

*"We craft legends together. Every Divine Crystal discovered, every soul choice made, every Void encounter survived—these echo across the cosmos. We trust each other to tell this story with respect, excitement, and creativity. When dice fall silent and sessions end, the tales we've woven will endure."*

Have each player (and you) affirm: *"I'm in."*

2. ## **2.2 Player Motivations and Epoch Calibration**

Understanding what draws each player ensures everyone gets moments to shine and stays engaged across dozens of sessions.

1. ### **The Four Player Archetypes**

**Combat Enthusiasts:** Love tactical positioning, damage optimization, and dramatic duels.

* **Feed them:** Divine Crystal weapons with unique properties, Voidborn monsters with interesting mechanics, House champions as rivals, mutation-enhanced enemies   
* **Powers they'll love:** War Channeling, Martial Channeling, Shadow Channeling (for assassin builds)   
* **Epoch progression:** Initiate \= learning to fight, Advanced \= legendary weapons, Ascended \= reality-warping combat 

**Roleplayers:** Crave character development, relationships, and moral choices.

* **Feed them:** Divine House intrigue, soul burning dilemmas, NPC relationships, character arcs, mutation roleplay   
* **Powers they'll love:** Dominion Channeling (control), Truth Channeling (social), Sanctuary Channeling (protection)   
* **Epoch progression:** Initiate \= personal stakes, Advanced \= House connections revealed, Ascended \= cosmic significance 

**Crafters/Builders:** Want to create, customize, and optimize gear and characters.

* **Feed them:** Divine Crystal crafting tables, Frame/SLD customization, Power combinations, base-building, Void-corrupted item experimentation   
* **Powers they'll love:** Creation Channeling, any Channeling Origin for Power combos   
* **Epoch progression:** Initiate \= scavenging shards, Advanced \= legendary crafting, Ascended \= reality forging 

**Explorers/Mystery Solvers:** Love discovering secrets, mapping the unknown, and solving puzzles.

* **Feed them:** Soulvegr gate mysteries, Tulip Freighter investigations, ancient ruins, Eternal revelations, Void zone exploration   
* **Powers they'll love:** Truth Channeling (investigation), Time Channeling (divination), Void Channeling (forbidden knowledge)   
* **Epoch progression:** Initiate \= rumors and fragments, Advanced \= major revelations, Ascended \= cosmic truth 

Most players are blends. Note each player's primary and secondary motivations.

2. ### **The Post-Session Zero Survey**

After Session Zero, send a quick survey (or ask verbally):

*"On a scale of 1-5 (1 \= not interested, 5 \= very excited), rate your interest in:"*

1. Divine Crystal crafting and legendary items   
2. Soul economy mechanics and moral choices (soul burning, resurrection)   
3. Void horror and cosmic dread (the Bloom, reality unmaking, mutations)   
4. Divine House politics and intrigue   
5. Combat encounters and tactical challenges   
6. Mystery investigation and lore discovery   
7. Character relationships and personal drama   
8. Exploration and new locations   
9. Character transformation (mutations, Voidborn evolution) 

Use results to weight your prep. If everyone rates "Divine Crystal crafting" as 4-5, make sure you have crafting opportunities. If "Void horror" averages 2, keep it minimal and always check in before intense scenes. If "character transformation" scores high, lean into mutation mechanics.

3. ### **Tailoring Epoch Progression**

**Initiate Epoch (Levels 1-10): Build personal stakes**

* Divine Crystals are rare rumors. Finding even a shard is a major victory.   
* Divine Houses are distant powers. Players might work for low-level operatives.   
* Soul economy is mysterious. SLDs are expensive luxuries.   
* The Bloom is a distant myth. Void mutations are rumors or affect NPCs only.   
* **Focus:** Survival, scavenging, establishing who these characters are 

**Advanced Epoch (Levels 11-20): Layer in cosmic connections**

* Divine Crystals become craftable if players invest in seeking them.   
* Divine Houses take direct interest. Mentors or Blood Awakenings reveal connections.   
* Soul burning becomes known (and tempting).   
* The Bloom becomes visible evidence (stars going dark).   
* Void mutations become a real threat to PCs.   
* **Focus:** Power, choice, and consequence 

**Ascended Epoch (Levels 21-30): Unleash cosmic agency**

* Divine Crystal matrices can be assembled (12-crystal constructions).   
* Divine Houses court or hunt the players as threats/assets.   
* Soul burning creates campaign-altering consequences (Echo armies, Void expansion).   
* The Bloom threatens entire sectors.   
* The Twelve Eternals can be discovered.   
* Voidborn transformations become possible.   
* **Focus:** Shaping reality, choosing sides in Creation vs. Destruction 

  4. ### **Adjusting Session Scoring**

Session Scoring (covered in detail in Section 3.3) can be weighted to reward what your table values:

**Combat-Heavy Table:** Award bonus AP for creative tactics, epic kills, defending allies

**Roleplay-Focused Table:** Award bonus AP for character development moments, meaningful choices, emotional scenes

**Mystery-Driven Table:** Award bonus AP for puzzle solutions, lore discoveries, connecting plot threads

**Craft-Focused Table:** Award bonus AP for successful Divine Crystal infusions, clever item use, base improvements

**Horror/Mutation Table:** Award bonus AP for surviving Void exposure, creative mutation roleplay, adapting to transformations

This ensures everyone progresses toward what excites them while maintaining the collaborative Soulvegr journey.

3. ## **2.3 Campaign Frameworks: Politics and Power**

Rather than creating everything from scratch, start with a framework and customize it to your table's interests. Here are five proven campaign structures:

1. ### **Framework 1: Crystal Wars (Crafting/Exploration)**

**Core Concept:** Rival Divine Houses contest control of Divine Crystal mines across multiple worlds.

**Primary Conflicts:**

* Mine raids and territory control   
* Crystal purity verification and authentication   
* Infusion rituals risking Void backlash   
* Rival scavenger teams and House operatives 

**Player Agency Tools:**

* Crystal purity rolls determine item quality   
* Relic creation with customizable properties   
* Mining rights can shift allegiances (bribe with Crystal shards)   
* Build bases near Crystal veins for long-term control 

**Epoch Evolution:**

* **Initiate:** Players scavenge Crystal fragments from ruins and battlefields. Even a single shard is valuable.   
* **Advanced:** Players can craft legendary items. Houses offer mentorship in exchange for mining rights or Crystal tithes.   
* **Ascended:** Players assemble full Crystal matrices (12-crystal constructions) capable of starship cores or reality anchors. 

**Sample Session:** House Veritas operatives hire the party to verify a suspected Veritite (Truth Crystal) vein in contested ruins. Rival House Umbra agents are already there. The vein is real but corrupted by Void exposure. Do players purify it (difficult), extract it corrupted (dangerous), or report it and let Houses fight?

2. ### **Framework 2: Soulvegr Raiders (Mystery/Economy)**

**Core Concept:** Intercept Tulip Freighters, navigate the Soul Cycle, and profit from (or reform) the soul economy.

**Primary Conflicts:**

* Freighter intercepts and cargo heists   
* Soul grading and authentication   
* Echo haunts from burned souls   
* Divine House reclaimers hunting stolen soul cargo   
* Moral questions: Is soul trading ethical? What about soul burning? 

**Player Agency Tools:**

* Soul burning grants immediate power but spawns Echoes   
* Players can become Soul Brokers (legal) or Soul Traffickers (illegal)   
* Resurrection services become a business opportunity   
* Navigate Soulvegr gates to find shortcuts or lost souls 

**Epoch Evolution:**

* **Initiate:** Players hear rumors of soul value. Maybe they're hired to transport soul shards without understanding what they are.   
* **Advanced:** Players learn soul burning mechanics and face temptation. Echo haunts become serious threats.   
* **Ascended:** Players can influence Soul Cycle policy, challenge Divine Houses' soul monopolies, or become Soul Judges themselves. 

**Sample Session:** A Tulip Freighter broadcasts a distress signal near a Soulvegr gate. Investigation reveals the crew has been murdered and the soul cargo stolen—but some souls remain trapped in the ship's damaged systems. Are they traumatized victims needing rescue, or dangerous Echoes? And who wants these specific souls badly enough to kill for them?

3. ### **Framework 3: Anchor Defenders (Survival/Horror)**

**Core Concept:** Stabilize reality against the Bloom's Void expansion using Divine Crystal anchors.

**Primary Conflicts:**

* Void incursions and unmaking waves   
* Reality erosion (memory loss, gear vanishing, mutations)   
* Divine Crystal anchor construction and maintenance   
* Voidborn entity invasions   
* Moral question: How much can you sacrifice to hold the line? 

**Player Agency Tools:**

* Anchor ritual mechanics (Crystal matrices hold back unmaking)   
* Failure consequences (gear erased, allies forgotten, history rewritten)   
* Crystal ward construction protects areas   
* Players choose which territories to save (and which to abandon)   
* Void mutations as ongoing threat and potential power source 

**Epoch Evolution:**

* **Initiate:** Void exposure is occasional and mysterious. Strange disappearances, reality flickers. NPCs show minor mutations.   
* **Advanced:** The Bloom becomes undeniable. Players must defend specific locations (cities, stations, planets). PC mutations become possible.   
* **Ascended:** The Bloom threatens entire sectors. Players might make galaxy-scale decisions about which civilizations to save. Voidborn transformations available. 

**Sample Session:** The frontier station Bastion-9 begins experiencing reality erosion. Objects vanish mid-use. People forget loved ones. A Void rift has opened in the station's reactor core. The only solution is a Crystal anchor ritual requiring a Divine Crystal—which the players don't have. They must choose: raid a nearby House facility for a Crystal (making powerful enemies), or evacuate and let 10,000 people be unmade.

4. ### **Framework 4: House Politics (Intrigue/Power)**

**Core Concept:** Navigate Divine House proxy wars, bid for influence, resist or embrace House edicts.

**Primary Conflicts:**

* House alliance shifts and betrayals   
* Realm of Influence territorial disputes   
* Proxy wars between House agents   
* Marriage alliances, assassination plots, blackmail   
* Moral question: Serve the system or tear it down? 

**Player Agency Tools:**

* Leverage tokens from Crystal control, favors, blackmail material   
* Edict resistance mechanics (saving throws against House commands)   
* Players can found their own House proxies or join existing ones   
* Political marriages, oath-binding rituals 

**Epoch Evolution:**

* **Initiate:** Players are pawns in House schemes, barely understanding the game.   
* **Advanced:** Players become valuable assets. Houses compete to recruit or eliminate them.   
* **Ascended:** Players can challenge House leadership, claim Godforge access, or reshape the Pantheon system. 

**Sample Session:** Two Divine Houses are on the brink of war over a disputed Divine Crystal mine. Both offer the party lucrative contracts to "resolve" the situation in their favor. But investigation reveals a third party—House Nullis (Void)—is secretly manipulating both sides, hoping the war will destabilize local reality enough for the Bloom to expand. Do players expose the manipulation, pick a side, or exploit the chaos for their own gain?

5. ### **Framework 5: Void Explorers (Science Horror/Discovery)**

**Core Concept:** Venture into Bloom-touched zones to study the Void, recover lost artifacts, and survive the mutations.

**Primary Conflicts:**

* Void zone expeditions (limited time before corruption)   
* Mutation management (beneficial vs. harmful changes)   
* Voidborn encounters (hostile natives vs. transformed survivors)   
* Corrupted gear and reality instability   
* Moral question: How much transformation is too much? 

**Player Agency Tools:**

* Mutation tables (roll results, adapt to changes)   
* Void-corrupted gear (powerful but unstable)   
* Mapping Void-touched zones (data \= credits)   
* Voidborn NPCs (potential allies or warnings) 

**Epoch Evolution:**

* **Initiate:** Short expeditions to Bloom edges. Minor mutations possible. Gear corruption begins.   
* **Advanced:** Deep dives into Void zones. Major mutations occur. Some PCs may seek Voidborn transformation deliberately.   
* **Ascended:** Living in Bloom-touched territories. Full Voidborn characters leading expeditions into the Deep Void itself. 

**Sample Session:** A mega-corporation hires the party to retrieve a pre-Bloom artifact from a newly-consumed research station. The station is now Void-touched—reality flickers, gravity is optional, time loops occur. The artifact is there, but so are the station's former crew... now Voidborn and territorial. Do players rush in, grab it, and leave (risking mutations), or negotiate with the Voidborn (who may not remember their former selves)?

8. ### **The Living Cosmos**

Your Soulvegr campaign should feel alive and responsive:

* **Crystal scarcity drives conflict:** When players secure a vein, rival factions notice   
* **Soul burning tempts corruption:** Every soul burned spawns Echoes and attracts attention   
* **Void growth threatens all:** The Bloom doesn't wait—it expands based on cosmic imbalance   
* **Divine Houses react:** They're not static—they scheme, ally, betray, and evolve

5. # **SECTION 3: RUNNING THE GAME**

Strong sessions flow from clear structure, confident rulings, and moments that linger in memory long after dice stop rolling. Section 3 equips you to orchestrate play that feels alive and collaborative, turning mechanics into memorable stories while weaving in Divine Crystal scarcity, soul burning risks, Void exposure, and mutations.

1. ## **3.1 Scene Structure and Pacing**

Great sessions have rhythm—peaks of excitement, valleys of tension-building, and satisfying resolutions. Use the **Hook-Tension-Payoff** structure for each major scene.

1. ### **The Three-Beat Scene**

**1\. Hook (What grabs attention?)**

Open every scene with a compelling image or question that immediately engages players:

**Strong Hooks:**

* A Divine Crystal vein pulsing with ethereal light in contested ruins   
* A Tulip Freighter broadcasting a distress beacon through Soulvegr storms   
* An Echo manifestation—translucent and screaming—materializing in the ship's corridor   
* House Veritas agents surrounding the party with weapons drawn: *"Hand over the Veritite shard. Now."*   
* Reality flickering—for a moment, your ally wasn't there. They don't remember either.   
* Your companion's eyes have turned completely black overnight. They haven't noticed yet. 

**Weak Hooks:**

* "You're in a room."   
* "Nothing happens yet."   
* "You travel for three days." (unless something interesting happens during travel) 

Ask yourself: *"If this were a TV show, would this scene make me lean forward or check my phone?"*

**2\. Tension (What raises the stakes?)**

Build tension through player choices, ticking clocks, and escalating complications:

**Timed Challenges:**

* *"Three rounds before Void exposure starts erasing your equipment."*   
* *"The Crystal infusion ritual takes 10 minutes—but House guards arrive in 8."*   
* *"Each hour in the Soulvegr gate, roll Spirit DC 20 or forget one ally."*   
* *"You have 1 hour before the mutation becomes permanent."* 

**Rising Complications:**

* Players secure the Divine Crystal... but it's corrupted by Void exposure   
* They successfully hack the door... but trigger an alarm   
* They negotiate peace... but a third faction attacks both sides   
* The ally who just mutated extra fingers can't wear their power armor anymore 

**Player-Driven Tension:**

* *"Do you burn the Flame-grade soul for \+20 AP immediately, knowing it will spawn an Echo?"*   
* *"The only way to stabilize the Anchor requires sacrificing a Divine Crystal you need for another quest."*   
* *"You can save the settlement OR retrieve the Crystal matrix—not both."*   
* *"Your mutation is spreading. Risk deeper Void exposure to find a cure, or accept the transformation?"* 

Tension comes from **meaningful choices with uncertain outcomes**, not arbitrary difficulty.

**3\. Payoff (What are the consequences?)**

Deliver consequences that reflect player decisions:

**Earned Victories:**

* Successful Divine Crystal infusion unlocks legendary weapon properties   
* Diplomatic resolution grants House alliance and Crystal mine access   
* Anchor stabilization saves the colony and earns their eternal gratitude   
* Successfully managing a mutation grants unexpected benefits 

**Costly Victories:**

* Crystal secured, but ally died holding the line   
* Soul burned for power, but Echo manifestation is now hunting you   
* Void rift closed, but you lost the memory of why you came here   
* Mutation prevented, but at the cost of a rare Divine Crystal 

**Defeats with Dignity:**

* Crystal lost to rivals, but you escaped with valuable intelligence   
* Anchor failed, territory unmade, but survivors remember your efforts   
* House arrests you, but your reputation prevents summary execution   
* Mutation occurred, but you adapt and find creative uses for your new form 

**Never rob players of agency.** Even defeats should flow from their choices, not GM fiat.

2. ### **Session Pacing**

**The 3-4 Hour Session Arc:**

**Opening (15-30 min):** Recap last session, establish current scene, Hook into immediate action

**Rising Action (60-90 min):** 2-3 major scenes with escalating Tension

* Alternate between action (Crystal heist, combat) and quieter moments (negotiations, planning, character development)   
* Use "spotlight rotation"—ensure every player gets at least one moment to shine 

**Climax (45-60 min):** Major decision point or encounter

* Divine Crystal infusion ritual under pressure   
* House confrontation with political stakes   
* Void incursion defense with reality at risk   
* Critical mutation crisis (save ally or let transformation complete) 

**Payoff & Cliffhanger (15-30 min):**

* Immediate consequences of the climax   
* Advancement Point discussion (if earned)   
* End on a compelling question: *"As the Anchor matrix cracks, you hear a voice from the Void: 'Finally... the seal weakens.' What do you do?"* 

  3. ### **Variety is Vital**

Don't run the same type of scene repeatedly. Alternate:

**Action Scenes:**

* Divine Crystal heists and extractions   
* Combat with Voidborn entities or rival House operatives   
* Chase sequences (ship-to-ship or on-foot)   
* Fighting while reality breaks down around you 

**Social Scenes:**

* House negotiations and political intrigue   
* Soul Broker dealings and moral dilemmas   
* Interrogations and investigations   
* Explaining mutations to horrified NPCs 

**Exploration Scenes:**

* Discovering new Soulvegr gates   
* Mapping Void-corrupted territories   
* Ancient ruins containing Divine Crystal fragments   
* Investigating mutation outbreaks 

**Ritual/Crafting Scenes:**

* Divine Crystal infusion ceremonies   
* Anchor matrix construction   
* Soul burning temptations with consequences   
* Attempting to reverse mutations 

**Character Moments:**

* Quiet conversations revealing backstory   
* Internal conflicts about soul burning   
* Relationships deepening or fracturing   
* Coming to terms with physical/mental changes 

If your last session was 80% combat, make the next one 60% investigation and negotiation.

4. ### **The Spotlight System**

Ensure every player gets meaningful moments:

**After a major success by one player, immediately spotlight another:**

*"Jax, your Divine Crystal infusion succeeds—the Genesyte pulses with green-gold light and bonds to your weapon. Zara, you're watching this with your psionic senses—what does the Creation Realm energy feel like to your mind?"*

*"Kaine, you've just negotiated an alliance with House Sanctuary. Ryn, your character has been quiet through these talks—what's going through their mind as they watch Kaine broker this deal?"*

*"Tam, your mutation just manifested—you've grown an extra set of eyes on the back of your head. Lyra, you notice this before Tam does. How do you tell them?"*

**Track spotlight mentally or with tokens:** If someone hasn't had a big moment in 30+ minutes, create one:

* *"Lyra, as the hacker, you notice something no one else would—the security system has a backdoor. What do you do?"*   
* *"Tam, your Life Channeling senses something wrong with the 'rescued' souls—they feel... hollow. Do you investigate?"*   
* *"Marcus, your new mutation (the extra finger) actually helps you here—you can trigger the mechanism no one else could reach. What do you do?"* 

  5. ### **Session Energy Management**

**Watch for fatigue signals:**

* Players checking phones frequently   
* Combat dragging with mechanical focus but no excitement   
* Discussions going in circles 

**When energy dips:**

* Take a 10-minute break   
* Inject a new complication or Hook   
* Skip to the interesting part: *"You spend three hours searching—what you find is..."*   
* Spotlight a different player 

**End on time.** Players with energy for "one more scene" will return eager. Players drained by marathon sessions may burn out.

2. ## **3.2 Adjudicating Rules and Edge Cases**

Your goal is confident, consistent rulings that keep play flowing while respecting the rules.

1. ### **Core Adjudication Principles**

**1\. Rules are Tools, Not Tyrants**

The rules support the story. When a rule interferes with fun or makes no narrative sense, adjust it.

**Good Ruling:** *"Normally that would require a full Action, but the situation is desperate and your description was awesome—make it a Bonus Action."*

**Bad Ruling:** *"I know it makes sense narratively, but the rules say you can't, so you can't."* (Unless it's game-breaking)

**2\. Consistency Over Perfection**

You don't need to know every rule perfectly. You need to rule consistently.

**When uncertain:**

* Make a ruling quickly   
* Note it for later verification   
* Apply the same ruling consistently for the rest of the session   
* Correct it next session if needed: *"Last session I ruled X, but checking the rules, it's actually Y. From now on, we'll use Y."* 

Players accept corrections far better than they accept mid-session rule debates.

**3\. Player Agency is Sacred**

**Never say:** *"That doesn't work"* without explanation.

**Instead say:**

* *"You can try, but the DC is very high because..."*   
* *"That would work if X, but currently Y is the obstacle..."*   
* *"Great idea\! Here's how we can make that work mechanically..."* 

  2. ### **Common Edge Cases in Soulvegr**

**Divine Crystal Crafting Conflicts:**

*"Can I use two Divine Crystals in one weapon?"*

**Ruling:** Yes, but each Crystal beyond the first increases the DC by \+5 and requires double the crafting time. Multiple Crystals can create powerful but unstable items.

**Soul Burning Questions:**

*"If I burn a soul for AP, can I immediately spend that AP on a Power I need right now in this fight?"*

**Ruling:** No. AP spending happens during advancement/rest phases, not mid-combat. However, you can burn the soul now and spend the AP after the combat ends (if you survive). The Echo consequences still apply.

**Void Exposure Ambiguity:**

*"The Void made me forget my ally exists—does that mean I attack them as an enemy?"*

**Ruling:** No. You simply don't remember they exist. They're not hostile—they're just... not there in your perception. You ignore them completely. Other players can try to convince you through roleplay (Spirit check DC 20).

**Mutation Timing:**

*"I just rolled a mutation that gives me extra fingers. Does that happen right now in combat or between sessions?"*

**Ruling:** Depends on severity and table preference (established in Session Zero). Minor mutations (extra finger, color-changing skin) can happen immediately for dramatic effect. Major mutations (extra limb, body horror) typically happen during rest/downtime unless the Void exposure was severe (then immediate, with appropriate warning).

**SLD Survival After 0 HP:**

*"My SLD's Survival DC is 18\. I'm at 0 HP. Do I roll the save now or after death saves?"*

**Ruling:** Immediately when reduced to 0 HP. If you succeed, your SLD remains intact and you make death saves normally. If you fail, True Death occurs—your soul cannot reconnect to any body.

**Power Interaction Questions:**

*"I cast Shadow Channeling's Step Through Shadow to teleport behind the enemy, then use War Channeling's Titanic Strike on the same turn. Does that work?"*

**Check the action economy:**

* Step Through Shadow: Bonus Action   
* Titanic Strike: Part of Attack Action 

**Ruling:** Yes, that works\! Bonus Action teleport, then Attack Action with the Power enhancement.

**Mutation Benefits in Combat:**

*"My mutation gave me an extra eye on the back of my head. Can I use that to avoid being surprised?"*

**Ruling:** Absolutely\! That's a reasonable benefit. You have 360° vision and cannot be surprised from behind. This is the "upside" of mutations—embrace the tactical advantages.

3. ### **The "Yes, And..." Principle**

When players propose creative solutions, reward ingenuity:

**Player:** *"Can I use my Creation Channeling to reshape the floor into spikes beneath the enemy?"*

**Bad Response:** *"That's not what Creation Channeling does."*

**Good Response:** *"That's awesome\! Make a Substance check DC 20\. Success creates difficult terrain and deals 2d6 damage to enemies in the area. Failure destabilizes the entire floor—everyone takes damage."*

**Player:** *"My mutation gave me a prehensile tail. Can I use it to grab the artifact while my hands are busy fighting?"*

**Bad Response:** *"That's not a free action."*

**Good Response:** *"Love it\! That's exactly the kind of creative mutation use I want to see. Make an Athletics check DC 15 to grab it while fighting."*

4. ### **Quick Ruling Flowchart**

Does the rules text explicitly cover this situation?  
├─ YES → Apply the rule as written  
└─ NO → Does it make narrative sense and is it cool?  
    ├─ YES → Allow it with appropriate DC/consequences  
    └─ NO → Explain why it doesn't work and offer alternative

3. ## **3.3 Session Scoring and Advancement**

End each session with collaborative scoring—a ritual that celebrates successes, acknowledges challenges, and tracks progression.

1. ### **The Five Categories**

Score each category 0-2 points (max 10 points per session):

**1\. Readiness (Preparation & Attendance)**

Did players come prepared to engage?

* **0 points:** Multiple no-shows, no one remembers what happened last session, character sheets missing   
* **1 point:** Everyone present, basic readiness, some tracking needed   
* **2 points:** All players present, character sheets updated, Divine Crystal inventory tracked, Void exposure logged, mutation changes noted, players know the current situation 

**Example Discussion:**  
*"Everyone showed up on time with sheets ready—that's 2 points. Great job tracking the Genesyte Crystal's purity level, Jax. And Marcus, I appreciate you noting your new mutation on your sheet."*

**2\. Accomplishment (Goals Achieved)**

Did the group make meaningful progress toward their goals?

* **0 points:** No significant progress, session felt directionless   
* **1 point:** Some progress, but major goals remain incomplete   
* **2 points:** Major objectives achieved—Divine Crystal secured, Soulvegr gate navigated, Anchor stabilized, House alliance forged, mutation crisis resolved 

**Example Discussion:**  
*"You infiltrated the House Veritas facility and retrieved the Veritite shard, even though you lost the secondary objective. That's meaningful progress—2 points."*

**3\. Teamwork (Collaboration)**

Did players support each other and work together?

* **0 points:** Party split, players competing, no coordination   
* **1 point:** Functional cooperation, some tactical coordination   
* **2 points:** Excellent support—crafters empowered warriors, psionics shielded allies from Echoes, players created opportunities for each other, party helped mutated ally adapt 

**Example Discussion:**  
*"Lyra's hacking gave Kaine the opening for a perfect ambush, and Tam immediately healed Ryn when things went south. Plus, when Marcus gained that mutation mid-fight, you all adapted your tactics to use his new abilities. That's teamwork—2 points."*

**4\. Immersion (Engagement & Atmosphere)**

Did the session feel compelling and engaging?

* **0 points:** Players distracted, combat mechanical and lifeless, no memorable moments   
* **1 point:** Generally engaged, some good moments   
* **2 points:** Highly engaged—Void unmaking created real tension, Divine House intrigue felt political and dangerous, players leaned in during key scenes, mutation reveal was intense 

**Example Discussion:**  
*"That moment when the Void started erasing Zara's memories and you all scrambled to help her remember—everyone was locked in. And when Tam discovered their mutation, the horror and adaptation was perfect. That Echo encounter had us all on edge. 2 points."*

**5\. Roleplay (Character Authenticity)**

Did players embody their characters and make choices that fit their personalities?

* **0 points:** Purely mechanical play, no character voice or motivation   
* **1 point:** Some character moments, occasional in-character decisions   
* **2 points:** Strong character portrayal—meaningful internal conflicts about soul burning vs. purity, relationships developed, characters made choices that cost them but fit who they are, mutation responses felt genuine 

**Example Discussion:**  
*"Kaine's struggle with whether to burn the Flame-grade soul was real. You knew it would help the group, but it violated your character's beliefs. That moment when you chose not to—perfect. And Jax's reaction when the Crystal infusion succeeded felt genuine. Marcus, your horror and then gradual acceptance of the mutation was beautifully played. 2 points."*

2. ### **Advancement Thresholds**

**Total Points → Advancement:**

When the group accumulates enough total points (tracked across sessions), everyone receives **Advancement Points (AP)**.

| Epoch | Points for 1 AP | Typical Sessions for Level |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Initiate (1-10)** | **10 points \= 1 AP** | **8-12 AP per level → \~2-3 sessions** |
| **Advanced (11-20)** | **12 points \= 1 AP** | **12-15 AP per level → \~3-4 sessions** |
| **Ascended (21-30)** | **15 points \= 1 AP** | **15-20 AP per level → \~4-5 sessions** |

**Example:**  
**Your Initiate Epoch party scores 8 points this session. They had 6 points carried over from last session. 6 \+ 8 \= 14 total points. That's enough for 1 AP (10 points), leaving 4 points toward the next AP.**

3. ### **Making It a Ritual**

Don't just announce points—discuss them:

**GM:** *"Alright, let's score the session. Readiness—everyone came prepared, sheets ready, you've been tracking the Crystals meticulously. I'm giving that 2 points. Agreed?"*

**Players:** *"Yeah\!"*

**GM:** *"Accomplishment—you secured the Veritite shard, but the House knows you took it now. Major progress with consequences. 2 points?"*

**Players:** *"Definitely."*

Continue through all five categories. This creates closure, acknowledges contributions, and reinforces what your table values.

4. ### **Tying AP to Story**

When players earn AP and want to spend it, connect their advancement to recent events:

**After Divine Crystal Infusion Success:**  
*"You've bonded with the Genesyte Crystal. It makes sense you'd unlock Creation Channeling Powers now—what do you take?"*

**After Soul Cycle Revelation:**  
*"You've seen how the Soul Cycle truly works. That trauma and knowledge has changed you. \+1 Spirit makes perfect sense."*

**After Void Horror Encounter:**  
*"Surviving that Void exposure fundamentally altered your perception of reality. You could increase Mind (understanding) or Spirit (resilience)—which fits better?"*

**After Mutation:**  
*"Your body has changed. You could put AP into adapting (increase Vitality), understanding the transformation (increase Mind), or embracing the new you (increase Spirit). What fits your character's response?"*

This transforms mechanical advancement into narrative growth.

5. ### **Bonus AP Awards**

Beyond the five categories, you can award bonus AP for exceptional moments:

* **Heroic Sacrifice:** Character risks True Death to save others (+1 AP)   
* **Brilliant Solution:** Player solves major problem in unexpected, narratively satisfying way (+1 AP)   
* **Compelling Roleplay:** Moment that had everyone at the table captivated (+1 AP)   
* **MVP Recognition:** Group vote on who carried the session (+1 AP)   
* **Creative Mutation Use:** Player finds genius tactical application for mutation (+1 AP) 

**Use sparingly**—these should feel special, not expected every session.

6. ### **Session Scoring Celebrates Cosmic Stakes**

Use scoring discussions to reinforce Soulvegr's themes:

*"You secured the Divine Crystal—that's progress toward legendary power."*

*"Burning that soul gave you the power to win, but the Echo consequences are coming. That choice will echo across sessions."*

*"You held the Anchor against the Void incursion. A whole settlement exists because of you."*

*"The House knows you betrayed them. That has weight."*

*"You survived Void exposure and gained a mutation. You're changed forever, but you're still you. That resilience matters."*

Make progression feel earned and meaningful, not just mechanical.

6. # **SECTION 4: BUILDING CHALLENGES**

Challenges drive Soulvegr stories forward, testing heroes against environments, foes, and cosmic forces while highlighting Divine Crystal scarcity, soul burning consequences, the Bloom's encroachment, and mutation risks. Section 4 provides scalable tools to craft encounters that feel fair, tense, and rewarding across all three Epochs.

1. ## **4.1 Encounter Design Principles**

Great encounters do more than drain HP—they present meaningful choices, showcase the unique elements of your setting, and create memorable moments.

1. ### **The Three Pillars of Soulvegr Encounters**

Design every major encounter around at least one (ideally two) of these pillars:

**1\. Threat (Traditional Challenge)**

* Combat encounters with enemies   
* Environmental hazards   
* Time-sensitive objectives   
* Skill challenges with failure consequences 

**2\. Divine Crystal Dependency (Resource Scarcity)**

* Legendary threats require Crystal-based solutions   
* Crystal acquisition drives risk-taking   
* Crystal purity affects success probability   
* Color resonance creates tactical choices 

**3\. Void Pressure (Existential Stakes)**

* Reality erosion and memory loss   
* Mutation mechanics that escalate   
* Unmaking mechanics that erase permanently   
* Anchor defense rituals   
* Consequences beyond death (gear erasure, history rewriting, physical transformation) 

  2. ### **Threat Budget System**

Use a simple budget to build balanced encounters:

**Budget Formula:** Group's Total Levels × 2 \= Encounter Budget

**Example:** 4 players at Level 5 \= (4 × 5\) × 2 \= 40-point budget

**Spending the Budget:**

| Element | Budget Cost | Example |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Basic Enemy** | **10 points** | **Scavenger, thug, minor beast** |
| **Elite Enemy** | **25 points** | **House operative, Crystal guardian** |
| **Boss Enemy** | **50+ points** | **Voidborn horror, House Champion** |
| **Divine Crystal Hunt** | **15-25 points** | **Finding and securing a Crystal shard** |
| **Void Exposure** | **20+ points** | **Reality erosion mechanics, memory loss, mutations** |
| **Complex Hazard** | **15 points** | **Collapsing mine, unstable reactor** |
| **Social Challenge** | **10-20 points** | **House negotiation, soul broker deal** |

**Difficulty Tiers:**

* **Easy Encounter:** Budget × 0.5 (good for after major battles, travel encounters)   
* **Moderate Encounter:** Budget × 1 (standard session challenge)   
* **Hard Encounter:** Budget × 1.5 (major setpiece, session climax)   
* **Deadly Encounter:** Budget × 2+ (should be rare, with retreat options) 

  3. ### **Divine Crystals as Key Resources**

Make Crystals matter mechanically:

**Legendary Threats Require Crystal Counters:**

* Void Bloom corruption needs Vitastone (Life Crystal) ward   
* House champions wielding Sovereignite (Dominion Crystal) require Liberite (Liberation Crystal) to counter   
* Reality-warping enemies require Temporalite (Time Crystal) stabilization 

**Color Resonance Tactics:**

* Using the "opposite" Crystal grants Advantage (Life vs. Death, Creation vs. Void, etc.)   
* Using a matching Crystal empowers the threat (don't use Void Crystals against Voidborn\!)   
* Neutral Crystals work but provide no special benefit 

**Crystal Scarcity Creates Choices:**

* *"We have one Aegisite (Sanctuary Crystal). Use it for the ward ritual now, or save it for the legendary armor we're planning?"*   
* *"This enemy is vulnerable to Conflictite (War Crystal), but we need that Crystal for the ship's weapons array."* 

  4. ### **Stakes Beyond HP**

**Traditional Stakes:** *"If we lose, we die."*

**Soulvegr Stakes:** *"If we lose, we're erased from existence and our friends forget we existed."* OR *"If we lose, we mutate beyond recognition."*

**Cosmic Consequences to Use:**

**Soul Burning Consequences:**

* Burned soul spawns Echo (immediate combat threat)   
* House Mortis (Death) enforcers hunt soul burners   
* Void expansion accelerates (reality becomes less stable) 

**Divine Crystal Failures:**

* Failed infusion shatters the Crystal (permanent resource loss)   
* Crystal backlash deals damage and attracts Void entities   
* Corrupted Crystals require dangerous purification rituals 

**Void Exposure Consequences:**

* **Gear unmade** (weapons/armor cease to have ever existed)   
* **Skills forgotten** (lose ranks permanently until restored)   
* **Allies erased from memory** (you don't remember they exist)   
* **Territory claimed by the Bloom** (entire planets unmade)   
* **Mutations occur** (physical/mental/magical transformation) 

**Anchor Failures:**

* Protected settlement is unmade   
* Void territory expands (permanent map change)   
* Reality becomes unstable (random effects persist) 

  5. ### **Encounter Type Variety**

Don't run the same encounter type repeatedly. Aim for this distribution across 4 sessions:

* **50% Combat-Focused:** Enemies, Voidborn, House operatives   
* **30% Crystal Acquisition/Crafting:** Hunts, infusions, purifications   
* **20% Void/Anchor Defense:** Reality stabilization, ritual protection, mutation management 

**Within a Single Session, Vary:**

* Divine Crystal heist (exploration \+ stealth)   
* Negotiations with House agents (social)   
* Voidborn ambush (combat)   
* Crystal infusion ritual under time pressure (skill challenge \+ combat)   
* Mutation crisis (character development \+ problem-solving) 

  6. ### **Dynamic Scaling**

Adjust challenges based on player choices:

**If players hoard Divine Crystals without using them:**

* House raiders attempt theft   
* Void entities attracted to Crystal concentrations   
* Opportunities that require spending Crystals 

**If players burn souls frequently:**

* Echo manifestations increase   
* House Mortis (Death) attention   
* Void expansion accelerates visibly   
* Moral consequences from NPCs 

**If players avoid Void-touched areas:**

* The Bloom expands, forcing confrontation   
* Refugees flee to player-controlled territory   
* Divine Houses offer lucrative contracts for Void missions 

**If players embrace mutations:**

* Divine Houses study them (recruitment or vivisection)   
* Other mutants seek them out (allies or enemies)   
* Mutation progression accelerates (more Void Exposure \= faster changes) 

**If players become too powerful:**

* Divine Houses take notice (recruitment or elimination)   
* Enemies with Crystal-based counters appear   
* Cosmic-scale threats emerge

   3. ## **4.3 Hazards, Puzzles, and Social Conflicts**

Not every challenge involves combat. Diversify with hazards, puzzles, and social encounters that highlight Soulvegr's unique mechanics.

1. ### **Divine Crystal Hazards**

**Unstable Crystal Infusion**

**Challenge:** Players attempt to infuse a Divine Crystal into a weapon/armor without proper preparation.

**Mechanics:**

* **DC:** 20 \+ (5 per additional Crystal beyond the first)   
* **Skill:** Substance \+ Crafting   
* **Success:** Item gains Crystal properties   
* **Failure (by 1-5):** Item works but has 15% malfunction chance   
* **Failure (by 6+):** Crystal shatters, deals 3d6 force damage to crafter and everyone within 10 ft   
* **Critical Failure:** Crystal backlash summons minor Void rift, everyone within 20 ft gains 1 Void Exposure Point 

**Assistance Options:**

* Mind-based analysis grants Advantage   
* Spirit-based Crystal resonance sensing grants \+2   
* Using proper facilities (workshop, House forge) reduces DC by 5 

**Crystal Vein Collapse**

**Challenge:** While mining a Divine Crystal vein, the structure becomes unstable.

**Mechanics:**

* **Round 1:** Tremors begin (warning)   
* **Round 2:** Debris falls (Force Save DC 15 or take 2d6 bludgeoning)   
* **Round 3:** Ceiling collapses (Force Save DC 18 or take 4d6 bludgeoning and become Restrained)   
* **Round 4:** Total collapse (anyone still inside takes 8d6 bludgeoning) 

**Solution Options:**

* **Evacuation:** Dash out (Athletics checks to navigate debris)   
* **Stabilization:** Substance check DC 20 to reinforce structure (requires tools and 2 rounds)   
* **Crystal Save:** Grab what you can and run (carry capacity matters) 

  2. ### **Void Hazards**

**Unmaking Waves**

**Challenge:** Cross a Void-corrupted zone where reality is actively eroding.

**Mechanics:**

**Duration Tracking:** Every hour in the zone requires Spirit Save DC 20

**Failure Progression:**

1. **First Failure:** Forget lowest Skill Rank (recoverable with Life or Sanctuary Crystal ritual)   
2. **Second Failure:** One ally erased from memory (Spirit DC 25 to remember them when reminded)   
3. **Third Failure:** Random gear unmade (weapon, armor, or important item ceases to exist)   
4. **Fourth Failure:** Roll on Minor Mutation table   
5. **Fifth+ Failure:** Personal history erased (lose Background benefits until restored) OR roll on Major Mutation table 

**Protection Options:**

* **Aegisite (Sanctuary Crystal) Ward:** Protects group for 1 hour, consumes shard   
* **Anchor Ritual:** Stabilizes 30 ft radius area (Substance check DC 25, requires 10 minutes, burns 1 Divine Crystal)   
* **Rush Through:** Move fast, accept consequences 

**Reality Fracture**

**Challenge:** Environment actively shifting and changing.

**Effects:**

* Walls appear/disappear randomly   
* Gravity fluctuates (fall "up" or sideways)   
* Time loops (repeat previous round)   
* Duplicates appear (Mirror images of party members—hostile)   
* Random mutations manifest in observers (temporary physical changes) 

**Resolution:**

* **Temporalite (Time Crystal) Stabilization:** Anchors timeline (Spirit check DC 22\)   
* **Navigate Through:** Mind checks DC 20 to predict shifts, move with the changes   
* **Embrace Chaos:** Use the instability tactically (trapping enemies in time loops, using gravity shifts for mobility) 

  3. ### **Soul Cycle Puzzles**

**Soulvegr Gate Navigation**

**Challenge:** Pass through a Soulvegr gate without losing your soul to the transit system.

**Mechanics:**

* **Insight Check DC 25:** Reveals safe path through soul-stream   
* **Failure:** Soul partially separates (1 level Exhaustion, Disadvantage on Spirit saves for 24 hours)   
* **Critical Failure:** Soul trapped in gate (requires rescue by rest of party) 

**Complications:**

* Echoes haunting the gate (from soul burning elsewhere)   
* Tulip Freighter passing through (risk of collection)   
* House Mortis (Death) patrols monitoring traffic 

**Freighter Manifest Decoding**

**Challenge:** Determine which souls aboard a Tulip Freighter are victims vs. willing participants.

**Mechanics:**

* **Investigation Check DC 20:** Decode manifest encryption   
* **Insight Check DC 22:** Determine soul origin from energy signature   
* **Truth Channeling Powers:** Auto-succeed with verification 

**Complications:**

* Some "willing" souls were coerced   
* Some "victim" souls are criminals who deserve their fate   
* Echoes of burned souls contaminate the manifest 

**Appeasing Echoes**

**Challenge:** Calm Echo manifestations without resorting to violence or soul burning.

**Mechanics:**

* **Roleplay Approach:** Learn what the Echo wants (usually justice, remembrance, or revenge for being burned)   
* **Persuasion/Deception Check DC varies:** Convince Echo their purpose is fulfilled   
* **Ritual Approach:** Perform soul purification (requires Aegisite or Mortisyte Crystal shard, Religion check DC 20\) 

**Complications:**

* Multiple Echoes with conflicting demands   
* Echo reveals uncomfortable truths about who burned them   
* Echo wants revenge on a party member 

  4. ### **Social Conflicts**

**Divine Crystal Trade Negotiation**

**Challenge:** Negotiate with a House representative or Soul Broker for Divine Crystal access.

**Mechanics:**

**Leverage System:** Earn tokens through Crystal purity verification, mine control, blackmail material, or favors

**3 Leverage Tokens \= Major Concession:** Reduced price, exclusive access, territorial rights

**Earning Leverage:**

* Demonstrate Crystal expertise (Crafting check DC 20\)   
* Provide valuable intelligence (Investigation or Insight)   
* Offer something they need (quest hook)   
* Intimidation or bribery (Authority or Substance checks) 

**Complications:**

* Rival bidder offers more   
* House has ulterior motive (tracking you, studying you)   
* Crystal is cursed/corrupted (intentionally or not) 

**Soul Burning Ethics Debate**

**Challenge:** The party faces a choice—burn a soul for immediate power, or refuse and face dire consequences.

**Setup:** Major threat approaching, party is outmatched, a high-grade soul is available

**Mechanics:**

* **This is not a skill check**—it's a character moment   
* Let players argue in-character   
* Implement consequences regardless of choice:   
  * **Burn Soul:** Gain power, defeat threat, Echo manifests later, moral weight   
  * **Refuse:** Harder battle, possible losses, maintain principles, potential rewards later 

**Complications:**

* Party splits on the decision (some want to burn, others refuse)   
* NPC witnesses react (respect or horror)   
* Divine House takes note (recruitment or censure) 

**Mutation Acceptance Challenge**

**Challenge:** An NPC (or PC) has mutated and the party must decide how to handle it.

**Setup:** Trusted ally shows signs of Void mutation (physical changes, behavioral shifts)

**Mechanics:**

* **This is primarily roleplay**, but Medicine/Mysticism checks DC 20 reveal mutation stage   
* **Options:**   
  * **Accept:** Ally remains, party adapts (potential benefits from mutation)   
  * **Cure:** Seek expensive Divine Crystal ritual (quest hook)   
  * **Isolate:** Quarantine ally (prevents spread but damages relationships)   
  * **Mercy Kill:** Prevent further transformation (dark but final) 

**Complications:**

* Mutation is progressing faster than expected   
* Other NPCs demand the mutated individual be exiled   
* Mutation grants unexpected useful abilities 

**House Marriage Alliance**

**Challenge:** Navigate a marriage proposal between party member and House scion (political tool).

**Mechanics:**

* **Benefits:** Crystal mine access, House protection, political leverage   
* **Costs:** Obligations, rival House hostility, personal freedom limited   
* **Resolution:** Deception to fake acceptance, Persuasion to negotiate terms, or simple acceptance/refusal 

**Complications:**

* Scion is actually sympathetic and doesn't want forced marriage either   
* Rival House attempts assassination at wedding   
* Marriage contract contains soul-binding clauses (literal magical binding) 

  5. ### **Universal Escalation Ladder**

Use this three-round structure for any challenge to maintain tension:

**Round 1: Opportunity Appears**

* Divine Crystal vein discovered   
* Social contact made   
* Puzzle reveals first clue   
* **Stakes:** Potential gain 

**Round 2: Complication Emerges**

* Void exposure begins (mutation risk)   
* Rival House reinforcements arrive   
* Time pressure introduced (countdown)   
* **Stakes:** Risk vs. reward 

**Round 3: Critical Decision**

* Successful infusion forges legendary relic OR failure shatters Crystal   
* Anchor ritual stabilizes reality OR collapse unmakes territory   
* Negotiation succeeds with concessions OR breaks down entirely   
* Mutation becomes permanent OR is prevented with sacrifice   
* **Stakes:** Permanent consequences 

**These tools ensure every challenge in Soulvegr reveals deeper cosmic machinery: Divine Crystal scarcity drives desperate choices, soul burning tempts power at terrible cost, Void growth forces impossible sacrifices, and mutations transform the unwary into something new.**

7. # **SECTION 5: THE DEEP LORE**

Soulvegr's universe pulses with hidden truths—ancient forces, Divine House rivalries, cosmic materials, and soul mechanics that players uncover layer by layer. Section 5 arms you with the full scope, from primordial architects to the Void's hunger, ready to reveal as campaigns deepen.

1. ## **5.1 The Twelve Eternals and the Cosmic Design**

**GM Directive: Extreme Secrecy.** The Twelve Eternals are the ultimate secret of the Soulvegr multiverse. Treat their existence as a campaign-shattering revelation, reserved for late Ascended Epoch via Godforge visions, cataclysmic Anchor ruptures, or direct contact with the eldest Divine House founders.

1. ### **What Are the Eternals?**

The **Twelve Eternals** are not gods—they are **primordial forces** that existed before reality itself. They are the fundamental laws that govern existence, personified as cosmic entities of incomprehensible power and alien perspective.

**They do not think like mortals.** They experience all of time simultaneously. They perceive reality across infinite dimensions. A single Eternal's "thought" might span millennia of mortal time.

**Even the Divine Houses don't fully understand them.** Most Divine House members believe they serve abstract ideals or ancestral traditions. Only the eldest, most powerful House founders have glimpsed the truth: they are instruments executing the Eternals' grand design.

2. ### **The Twelve Eternals and Their Realms**

Each Eternal embodies one of the **12 Realms of Influence** and serves a specific cosmic function:

| Eternal | Realm | Domain | Cosmic Function |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **The Chronarch** | **Eternity** | **Timelessness, endurance, permanence** | **Preserves what must not change; anchors reality against temporal chaos** |
| **The Revealer** | **Truth** | **Knowledge, honesty, revelation** | **Ensures information flows; prevents total ignorance or deception** |
| **The Unseen** | **Shadow** | **Secrets, concealment, hidden paths** | **Protects necessary mysteries; prevents total transparency** |
| **The Architect** | **Creation** | **Formation, matter, construction** | **Births new realities; shapes raw potential into form** |
| **The Lifebringer** | **Life** | **Vitality, growth, renewal** | **Sustains existence; ensures the cycle continues** |
| **The Timekeeper** | **Time** | **Flow, causality, sequence** | **Maintains cause and effect; prevents temporal paradox** |
| **The Warden** | **Sanctuary** | **Protection, refuge, preservation** | **Shields the vulnerable; creates safe havens** |
| **The Conqueror** | **War** | **Conflict, testing, strength** | **Forges excellence through struggle; prevents stagnation through comfort** |
| **The Nullifier** | **Void** | **Entropy, unmaking, reset** | **Consumes imbalance; erases what should not exist** |
| **The Reaper** | **Death** | **Endings, harvest, conclusion** | **Ensures all things end; prevents immortal stagnation** |
| **The Liberator** | **Liberation** | **Freedom, breaking bonds, change** | **Prevents eternal tyranny; enables transformation** |
| **The Sovereign** | **Dominion** | **Control, hierarchy, order** | **Establishes structure; prevents total chaos** |

3. ### **The Cosmic Balance**

The Eternals exist in **six paired opposites**, each pair maintaining equilibrium:

**1\. Eternity ↔ Time**

* *Eternity* preserves permanence; *Time* enforces change   
* **Imbalance:** Too much Eternity \= static universe; too much Time \= reality crumbles from constant flux 

**2\. Truth ↔ Shadow**

* *Truth* reveals all; *Shadow* conceals what must stay hidden   
* **Imbalance:** Too much Truth \= no privacy, no mystery, existential despair; too much Shadow \= ignorance, deception, lost knowledge 

**3\. Creation ↔ Void**

* *Creation* forms reality; *Void* unmakes what shouldn't exist   
* **Imbalance:** Too much Creation \= chaotic overgrowth, resource depletion; too much Void \= total annihilation, the Bloom consumes all 

**4\. Life ↔ Death**

* *Life* sustains and grows; *Death* ends and harvests   
* **Imbalance:** Too much Life \= immortal crowding, resource collapse; too much Death \= extinction, empty void 

**5\. Sanctuary ↔ War**

* *Sanctuary* protects and preserves; *War* tests and strengthens   
* **Imbalance:** Too much Sanctuary \= weakness, stagnation; too much War \= endless destruction, no recovery 

**6\. Liberation ↔ Dominion**

* *Liberation* frees and changes; *Dominion* controls and stabilizes   
* **Imbalance:** Too much Liberation \= chaos, no structure; too much Dominion \= tyranny, no growth 

  4. ### **The Eternal Design**

The Eternals created the **Cosmic Design**—a self-regulating system to maintain balance:

**The Godforge:** Ultimate tool of Creation. Allows worthy mortals to ascend, claim Realm authority, and shape reality. Creates new gods, new worlds, new possibilities. **Accessing it is nearly impossible and invites cosmic opposition.**

**The Void:** Ultimate tool of Destruction. Consumes imbalance, unmakes failures, and resets cosmic errors. The Bloom is the Void's current manifestation.

**Divine Houses:** Organizations (not bloodlines, though some claim heritage) established by the Eternals' first champions. They embody the 12 Realms and unknowingly execute the Eternals' will.

**Divine Crystals:** Physical manifestations of Eternal power. They enable certain advanced creation (specific legendary items, ship-building, reality anchors) and enhance all legendary crafting. Mining them without allowing natural reformation accelerates the Bloom.

**Soul Cycle:** Souls are the backbone of reality—the fundamental energy that sustains the cosmic system. The cycle of death → collection → processing → recycling maintains cosmic equilibrium. Soul burning bypasses this cycle, creating imbalance.

**The Bloom (Entropy Wave):** The Void's response to imbalance. When Creation outpaces Destruction (too much soul burning, too much Crystal mining, too much immortality), the Void expands to restore balance.

5. ### **Why the Eternals Remain Hidden**

**1\. They Don't Think Like Mortals**

Attempting to comprehend an Eternal's true nature risks madness. They exist across all timelines simultaneously. A conversation with an Eternal is not linear—they respond to questions you haven't asked yet and reference events that haven't happened.

**2\. Direct Contact is Catastrophic**

**Witnessing an Eternal requires Spirit Save DC 30:**

**Success:** You glimpse cosmic truth but:

* Gain 1 permanent level of Exhaustion   
* Receive Realm-specific curse (creativity drains if you witness The Architect, memories fade if you witness The Timekeeper, etc.)   
* Your soul grade permanently increases by 1 (you matter more cosmically now)   
* Divine Houses become aware of you 

**Failure:** Permanent madness:

* You can function but reality never quite makes sense again   
* Random hallucinations of Eternal machinery   
* Temporal displacement (you answer questions before they're asked)   
* Cannot distinguish past/present/future reliably   
* Other Realms bleed into your perception (see shadows of Truth, hear whispers of Void) 

**3\. They Use Proxies**

Eternals work through:

* **Divine Houses** (instruments executing their will)   
* **Divine Crystals** (physical manifestations of power)   
* **Cosmic phenomena** (the Bloom, Soulvegr gates, Void rifts)   
* **Visions and omens** (glimpses granted to mortals with purpose) 

**4\. Knowledge is Dangerous**

Divine Houses hunt anyone who learns about the Eternals. Not because the Houses are evil—because knowledge of the Eternals tends to drive people to:

* Attempt to contact them (catastrophic)   
* Try to disrupt the Design (suicidal)   
* Burn souls seeking Eternal power (accelerates the Bloom)   
* Challenge the Houses directly (destabilizes reality) 

  6. ### **When to Reveal the Eternals**

**Initiate Epoch (Levels 1-10):** Never. Players should not even hear rumors.

**Advanced Epoch (Levels 11-20):** Vague hints only

* Ancient texts reference "primordial forces"   
* Dying Divine House elder whispers cryptic warnings   
* Void visions show incomprehensible geometries   
* Divine Crystals occasionally "pulse" as if alive 

**Ascended Epoch (Levels 21-30):** Direct revelation possible

* Godforge visions reveal the Design   
* Anchor collapse creates Eternal contact moment   
* Divine House founder shares the truth   
* Reality-warping event makes the machinery visible 

  7. ### **Revelation Methods**

Player(s) attempting to access the Godforge receive a vision of the Cosmic Design—twelve forces in perfect balance, maintaining all existence. Spirit Save DC 25 or take 1 level Exhaustion from the enormity.

**Anchor Rupture:**  
When a major Anchor fails, reality thins enough to glimpse beyond. For one round, players see the Eternals' machinery—soul-streams flowing, Divine Crystals pulsing with Eternal energy, the Void consuming imbalance.

**Divine House Confession:**  
A House founder, on their deathbed or in desperate circumstances, shares the truth: *"We are not rulers. We are instruments. The Twelve Eternals use us to maintain balance. The Godforge is their gift. The Void is their judgment."*

8. ### **The Eternals' Goals**

**They want:**

* Balance maintained (Creation ↔ Void, Life ↔ Death, etc.)   
* The Cosmic Design to function   
* Worthy mortals to ascend and claim Realm authority (preventing stagnation)   
* Failed systems to be unmade (the Bloom consuming corrupt civilizations) 

**They don't care about:**

* Individual mortal lives   
* Mortal concepts of "good" and "evil"   
* Divine House politics   
* Whether specific civilizations survive 

**They will intervene if:**

* The balance tips catastrophically (one Eternal becomes too dominant)   
* The Godforge is threatened   
* An entity attempts to destroy the Design itself

  4. ## **5.4 Soul Cycle \- Life, Death, Soulvegr, and Burning**

The Soul Cycle is the backbone of reality—the fundamental energy system that sustains the cosmos. Most people know fragments of the truth. You know it all.

1. ### **The True Soul Cycle (Five Stages)**

**Stage 1: Mortal Death**

* Body dies, soul separates   
* Soul crystallizes based on quality: **Ember** (weakest) → **Spark** → **Flame** → **Blaze** → **Inferno** (strongest)   
* Natural astral cord severs OR SLD attempts survival roll   
* **If SLD survives:** soul remains anchored, can be resleeved into new body   
* **If SLD fails or no SLD:** soul enters Stage 2 

**Stage 2: Soulvegr Transit**

* Soul drifts toward nearest Soulvegr gate (invisible pathways between planes)   
* **Tulip Freighters**—massive living soul-ships—patrol these gates, collecting souls   
* Transit takes 1-7 days depending on distance from gate   
* Souls experience the journey consciously (awareness of being "collected") 

**Stage 3: Pantheon Processing**

* Tulip Freighters deliver souls to Divine House Mortis (Death Realm) processing centers   
* Souls are **graded**: Ember → Spark → Flame → Blaze → Inferno (based on **cosmic significance**, not morality)   
* High-grade souls (Flame+) are contested—multiple Houses bid for rights   
* Graded souls are allocated to appropriate uses 

**Stage 4: Recycling (The Intended Path)**

* **Ember souls:** Fuel basic Divine House operations, minor Crystal formation   
* **Spark souls:** Power mid-level Channeling, fuel Ley-Drives, form Crystal veins   
* **Flame souls:** Empower legendary crafting, fuel major edicts, strengthen Anchors   
* **Blaze souls:** Power reality-shaping, fuel Godforge rituals, create new Divine Crystals   
* **Inferno souls:** Ascension fuel, create new Divine House champions, stabilize collapsing realities 

**This is the backbone of reality—souls sustaining the cosmic system through proper cycling.**

**Stage 5A: Soul Burning (The Corruption)**

* **Direct consumption** of souls for immediate power   
* **Bypasses** Pantheon processing \= **imbalance**   
* Grants instant Advancement Points (AP)   
* Spawns **Echoes**—vengeful soul remnants denied proper cycling   
* **Accelerates the Bloom** (Void expansion) 

**Stage 5B: Reincarnation (Rare)**

* Occasionally, processed souls are returned to the cycle as new mortals   
* Most common with Ember/Spark souls (not valuable enough to fully consume)   
* Memories erased, but soul essence remains   
* Explains "old souls" and prodigies (Flame-grade soul reincarnated as mortal) 

  2. ### **Soul Grading System**

| Soul Grade | Cosmic Value | Common Sources | Typical Uses |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Ember** | **Minimal** | **Most mortals, basic lives** | **Fuel for basic operations, minor Crystal growth** |
| **Spark** | **Low** | **Skilled individuals, minor heroes** | **Channeling fuel, Ley-Drive power, vein formation** |
| **Flame** | **Moderate** | **Exceptional mortals, minor legends** | **Legendary crafting, major edicts, Anchor fuel** |
| **Blaze** | **High** | **Heroes, Divine House operatives, major figures** | **Reality-shaping, Godforge components, Crystal creation** |
| **Inferno** | **Extreme** | **Divine House champions, legendary heroes** | **Ascension fuel, stabilize cosmic crises, new god creation** |

**Beyond Inferno (Hidden Grades):**

The Player's Handbook hints at grades beyond Inferno. Here's the truth:

**Supernova (Grade 6):** Ascended beings, Divine House founders. Used only in cosmic emergencies.

**The Seven Cosmic Grades (Forbidden Knowledge):** Connected to the Eternals themselves. Learning their names causes madness. Only the eldest Divine House founders know they exist.

3. ### **Soul Burning Consequences**

When a character burns a soul for power, use this table:

| Soul Grade | AP Gained | Echo Manifestation | Divine House Response | Void Impact |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Ember** | **\+1 AP** | **Nightmares (Disadvantage on Spirit checks for 3 days)** | **None** | **Negligible** |
| **Spark** | **\+5 AP** | **Physical Echo (1d6 psychic damage/night for 1 week)** | **House Mortis monitors** | **Minor (local reality flickers)** |
| **Flame** | **\+20 AP** | **Soul-Scar Hindrance (permanent until removed by ritual)** | **House Mortis dispatches soul-hunters** | **Moderate (Bloom grows slightly)** |
| **Blaze** | **\+50 AP** | **Echo Swarm (encounter with 2d6 Echoes within 1d4 days)** | **Pantheon edict issued against burner** | **Significant (Anchor destabilization)** |
| **Inferno** | **\+100 AP** | **Reality Fracture (Void rift opens near burner's location)** | **Multiple Houses pursue, Eternal judgment** | **Catastrophic (local Bloom acceleration)** |

**Soul-Scar Hindrance:**

* Cannot be removed by normal means   
* Requires ritual: Aegisite (Sanctuary Crystal) \+ Mortisyte (Death Crystal) \+ 7-day ceremony   
* Grants Echoes easier access to burner   
* Divine Houses can sense soul-scarred individuals 

  4. ### **Echoes: Vengeful Remnants**

Echoes are what's left when souls are burned instead of properly cycled:

**Echo Statistics (Typical):**

* **Attributes:** Spirit 14, Mind 12, Force 8   
* **HP:** 15-25 (depending on original soul grade)   
* **DV:** 12-14   
* **Skills:** Varies based on soul's former life   
* **Attacks:** Soul Drain touch (1d8 psychic damage, reduces max HP by damage dealt until Long Rest)   
* **Special:** Incorporeal (Resistance to physical damage, immune to non-magical weapons)   
* **Weakness:** Vulnerable to Aegisite (Sanctuary) and Mortisyte (Death) Crystal weapons, radiant damage 

**Echo Behavior:**

* Obsessively hunt the person who burned them   
* Attack anyone protecting the burner   
* Cannot rest until burner is dead OR soul is ritually purified   
* Multiple Echoes from same burner will cooperate   
* Echoes can possess corpses temporarily (creating undead) 

**Echo Swarms:**  
Burning Blaze+ souls creates swarms (2d6 Echoes). They coordinate attacks, share information telepathically, and won't stop until the burner dies.

5. ### **Tulip Freighters: The Soul Ships**

**What They Are:**

* Living ships grown from bio-organic matter and soul-crystal matrices   
* **Size:** Moon-sized (some larger)   
* **Appearance:** Massive petal-like structures that "bloom" when collecting souls   
* **Sentience:** Semi-aware, but obedient to Divine House Mortis pilots 

**How They Work:**

* Patrol Soulvegr gates constantly   
* Souls are drawn to them magnetically (cannot resist)   
* Internal chambers store thousands of souls in crystalline matrices   
* Transit routes follow ancient patterns (predictable if you know where to look) 

**Crew:**

* **Soul Shepherds:** Divine House Mortis priests who guide collection   
* **Reapers:** Enforcers who ensure proper soul handling   
* **Graders:** Specialists who assess soul quality   
* **Pilots:** Navigate Soulvegr currents 

**Security:**

* Heavily warded against intrusion   
* Void-touched beings cannot approach (Sanctuary Crystal barriers)   
* Divine House Mortis authority allows instant kill of trespassers   
* Some Freighters are guarded by Echo swarms (controlled, not wild) 

**Why Raid Them?**

* Access to high-grade souls   
* Steal soul cargo for burning (or legitimate resurrection)   
* Rescue specific individuals before processing   
* Disrupt Divine House Mortis operations   
* Discover Soulvegr gate locations 

**Consequences of Raiding:**

* Divine House Mortis declares you soul-thieves (death sentence)   
* Soul Cycle disruption (local Bloom acceleration)   
* Echo manifestations from disturbed souls   
* Other Divine Houses may support Mortis (system stability matters) 

  5. ## **5.5 The Void \- Nature, Growth, Contents, and Terror**

The Void is not empty space. It is **hyper-dense unmaking**—a realm where entropy collects and failed realities go to die. It is the Eternals' tool of Destruction, balancing the Godforge's Creation.

1. ### **What the Void Actually Is**

**Common Misconception:** The Void is nothingness, absence, empty space between stars.

**Truth:** The Void is **hyper-compressed entropy**. It contains:

* **Echo Remnants:** Burned souls denied Soulvegr cycling   
* **Unmade Matter:** Raw potential that was created but failed to stabilize   
* **Voidborn Entities:** Beings native to entropy (not invaders—they belong there)   
* **Failed Realities:** Pocket dimensions that collapsed   
* **Corrupted Throne Shards:** Fragments of failed Godforge attempts   
* **Erased Histories:** Events that were unmade retroactively   
* **Mutated Beings:** Mortals transformed by prolonged Void exposure 

**Physical Properties:**

* Light doesn't escape (appears as absolute blackness)   
* Time doesn't flow normally (cause can follow effect)   
* Physics breaks down (gravity inverts, matter phases)   
* Consciousness erodes (memory loss, identity dissolution)   
* **Biology mutates** (physical/mental/magical transformation) 

  2. ### **The Bloom (Entropy Wave): Void Expansion**

**What It Is:**  
The Void's response to cosmic imbalance. When Creation outpaces Destruction, the Void expands to restore equilibrium.

**Current Triggers (Why the Bloom Grows):**

1. **Soul Burning \> Soul Recycling:** Too many souls burned instead of properly cycled   
2. **Divine Crystal Mining \> Crystal Formation:** Extracting Crystals faster than new veins grow   
3. **Immortality Proliferation:** SLD technology preventing natural death cycle   
4. **Pantheon Imbalance:** Realms out of alignment (Liberation suppressed, Dominion dominant)   
5. **Anchor Failures:** Crystal matrices destabilizing, unable to hold back Void 

**Progression:**

**Initiate Epoch (Early Signs):**

* Stars on galactic edge go dark (not exploding—vanishing)   
* Occasional reality flickers in Frontier zones   
* Strange dreams and visions   
* Minor items occasionally cease to exist (initially blamed on theft/loss)   
* Rare mutation reports in Bloom-adjacent zones (dismissed as rumors) 

**Advanced Epoch (Visible Threat):**

* Entire systems unmade (planets, stars, all gone)   
* Soulvegr gates malfunction (souls lost in transit)   
* Frontier settlements evacuated or abandoned   
* Void-touched zones expand (areas where reality is thin)   
* Voidborn sightings increase   
* **Mutations become common** in exposed populations 

**Ascended Epoch (Existential Crisis):**

* The Bloom threatens the Spires (Core Worlds at risk)   
* Anchor collapses become common   
* Divine Houses openly war over remaining stable territory   
* Mass exodus from Bloom-adjacent sectors   
* Reality itself becomes negotiable in affected zones   
* **Voidborn populations** (transformed survivors) establish territories 

**Reversal Methods:**

* Vitastone (Life Crystal) Rituals: Large-scale renewal ceremonies   
* Balanced Pantheon Edicts: All 12 Realms working in concert   
* Anchor Matrix Reconstruction: 12-Crystal Anchors to stabilize reality   
* Soul Cycle Restoration: Stop soul burning, increase proper recycling   
* Godforge Intervention: Ultimate solution (requires Ascended champions) 

  3. ### **Void Exposure and Mutation**

**The Void has three proximity zones:**

**Zone 1: Inside the Void (Instant Destruction)**

* Unprotected exposure \= atomic disintegration within rounds   
* Protected (Sanctuary Crystal ward, specific Powers, Void-Touched physiology) \= survivable but extremely dangerous   
* **This is NOT where mutations happen**—you just die 

**Zone 2: Bloom Edge (Void-Touched Territory)**

* Reality is thin here   
* Gravity fluctuates, physics breaks down   
* **THIS IS WHERE MUTATIONS HAPPEN**   
* Time spent here without protection \= gradual corruption   
* **Duration:** Hours to days depending on proximity 

**Zone 3: Bloom-Adjacent (Relatively Safe)**

* Stars have gone dark, reality feels "wrong"   
* Strange phenomena but not immediately dangerous   
* **Minimal mutation risk** unless prolonged exposure (weeks/months) 

  4. ### **Void Exposure Tracking**

Track **Void Exposure Points (VEP)** when characters spend time in Zone 2 (Bloom Edge):

**Gaining VEP:**

* 1 VEP per hour in Bloom Edge zones (Spirit Save DC 20 negates)   
* 1 VEP per round of direct Void contact WITH protection (instant death without)   
* 1 VEP when taking 20+ damage from Void sources 

**VEP Thresholds:**

* **3 VEP:** Roll on Minor Mutation Table (d100)   
* **6 VEP:** Roll on Major Mutation Table (d100)   
* **10 VEP:** Voidborn Transformation (special transformation, character still playable) 

  5. ### **Void Exposure Effects (Without Mutation)**

| Duration | Effect | Save DC | Recovery Method |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **1 Round** | **\-2 to all d20 rolls** | **None** | **Long Rest** |
| **1 Minute** | **Forget 1 Skill Rank (lowest)** | **Spirit DC 20** | **Spirit DC 25 or Vitastone (Life) Crystal ritual** |
| **10 Minutes** | **Random non-magical item unmade** | **Spirit DC 22** | **Cannot be recovered (item never existed)** |
| **1 Hour** | **1 Ally erased from memory** | **Spirit DC 25** | **Vitastone (Life) Crystal ritual DC 30 or ally convinces you** |
| **1 Day** | **Personal history partially unmade (lose Background benefits)** | **Spirit DC 28** | **Aegisite (Sanctuary) Anchor ritual** |
| **Permanent** | **Retroactive non-existence** | **Spirit DC 35** | **Godforge intervention only** |

**Protection Methods:**

* **Aegisite (Sanctuary Crystal) Wards:** Create 30 ft safe zone for 1 hour (consumes shard)   
* **Anchor Matrices:** Stabilize reality in larger areas (requires 12-Crystal matrix)   
* **Mental Discipline:** Spirit saves resist effects   
* **Quick Exit:** Leave before severe exposure accumulates   
* **Void Channeling Origin:** Grants Advantage on saves 

  6. ### **Void Terror Mechanics**

The Void doesn't just kill—it unmakes. Use these horror elements:

**Unmaking (Not Death):**

* Character hits 0 HP from Void damage → They weren't in this scene   
* Allies don't remember them being there   
* Their equipment was "always in storage"   
* Social connections remain but memories are confused 

**Physics Failure:**

* Gravity inverts randomly (fall "up")   
* Causality reverses (effect before cause: you're injured before the attack lands)   
* Geometry breaks (rooms have more corners inside than outside)   
* Time loops (repeat same 6 seconds endlessly) 

**Memory Theft:**

* Forget why you came here   
* Forget your companions' names (they're standing right there, but who are they?)   
* Forget your own purpose (why do I carry this weapon? What am I good at?)   
* Forget you're being affected (no awareness of memory loss) 

**Identity Erosion:**

* Your reflection doesn't match you   
* Your voice sounds wrong   
* Your body doesn't feel like yours   
* You remember being someone else 

  7. ### **Voidborn Entities**

Unlike demons or aliens, Voidborn are **native** to entropy. They don't invade—they exist where the Void is.

**Types:**

**Void Wraiths (Common):**

* Appear as silhouettes of darkness   
* Drain memories with touch   
* Phase through matter   
* **Weakness:** Cannot exist in areas with strong Aegisite (Sanctuary) presence 

**Amalgams (Advanced):**

* Formed from multiple unmade entities fused together   
* Constantly shifting shape   
* Consume Divine Crystals to evolve   
* Emit unmaking fields (30 ft radius, items randomly vanish) 

**Nullifiers (Rare):**

* Humanoid-shaped absence   
* Cancel all Powers/magic in 60 ft radius   
* Touch unmakes matter (instant kill if Vitality Save fails)   
* Extremely intelligent, can speak (though words hurt to hear) 

**The Unremembered (Legendary):**

* Entities so thoroughly unmade they exist only as absences   
* You forget them as soon as you stop looking   
* Attacking them requires fighting something you can't remember   
* Kill by making you cease to have existed 

**Transformed Voidborn (Mortals):**

* Former mortals who accumulated 10+ VEP and survived   
* Can exist in both Void zones and normal reality   
* Visibly altered (see Voidborn Transformation table in Section 6\)   
* May retain memories and personality (or may not)   
* Potential allies, enemies, or warnings of what's to come 

  8. ### **Void Growth Triggers & Shrinkage Methods**

**What Accelerates the Bloom:**

* Soul burning (especially Flame+ souls)   
* Excessive Divine Crystal mining   
* Pantheon imbalance (missing Realms)   
* Anchor failures   
* Mass immortality (SLDs preventing natural death cycle)   
* Reality-warping magic without safeguards   
* Godforge access attempts by unworthy 

**What Slows the Bloom:**

* Vitastone (Life Crystal) renewal rituals   
* Proper Soul Cycle adherence (recycling \> burning)   
* Balanced Pantheon edicts (all 12 Realms represented)   
* Anchor matrix construction and maintenance   
* Allowing natural death to occur   
* Divine Crystal vein conservation (controlled mining) 

**Permanent Reversal:**  
Only possible through Godforge intervention with balanced 12-Realm authority.

6. ## **5.6 Soul Economy, Tulip Freighters, and Cosmic Balance**

The soul economy is the hidden financial system of the multiverse. Souls are currency, commodity, and cosmic fuel all at once.

1. ### **Soul Economy Basics**

**What Souls Enable:**

* **Resurrection:** Flame-grade soul \= full restoration   
* **Divine Crystal Crafting:** Spark+ souls bind Crystals to items   
* **Ley-Drive Fuel:** Freighters powered by recycled Ember souls   
* **Power Origin Access:** Advanced Backgrounds grant Origins via soul-trade   
* **Advancement:** Soul burning grants AP (illegal but effective)   
* **Edict Enforcement:** Divine Houses burn souls to enforce cosmic law   
* **Reality Anchors:** Blaze souls stabilize collapsing territories 

**Legal vs. Illegal:**

**Legal Soul Trade (Sanctioned by Divine House Mortis):**

* Resurrection contracts (you pay now, they resurrect you if you die)   
* Soul insurance (family receives compensation if you experience True Death)   
* Voluntary soul donation (you die, your soul goes to specific cause)   
* Estate soul-wills (allocate your soul's use after death) 

**Illegal Soul Trade (Black Market):**

* Soul burning for AP   
* Forced soul harvesting   
* Soul trafficking (kidnapping for soul extraction)   
* Echo creation for sale (weaponized Echoes)   
* Unregulated resurrection (no Divine House oversight) 

  2. ### **Tulip Freighters: Deep Dive**

**Operating Schedule:**

* Patrol routes are predictable (if you have star charts and House Mortis intelligence)   
* Collection happens every 1-7 days at each Soulvegr gate   
* Freighters can be tracked via soul-energy signatures (requires Mysticism Rank 8+) 

**Cargo Value:**  
A single Freighter might carry:

* 10,000+ Ember souls (1 AP each if burned \= 10,000 AP total—catastrophic if stolen)   
* 500+ Spark souls   
* 50+ Flame souls   
* 5-10 Blaze souls   
* 0-2 Inferno souls (rare) 

**Why This Matters:**  
Raiding a Tulip Freighter could grant enough souls to:

* Instantly advance an entire party multiple levels (through burning—suicidal)   
* Fund a major Divine House operation   
* Crash the local soul economy   
* Trigger massive Echo swarms   
* Accelerate the Bloom catastrophically 

**Divine House Mortis Response to Theft:**

* Death sentence (executed on sight, no trial)   
* Soul-hunter deployment (elite operatives with Death Channeling)   
* Pantheon edict support (all Houses cooperate against soul-thieves)   
* Echo weaponization (controlled Echoes released to hunt thieves) 

  3. ### **Soul Brokers: The Middlemen**

**What They Do:**

* Facilitate legal soul trades   
* Authenticate soul quality (grading verification)   
* Arrange resurrection contracts   
* Provide soul storage (Mortisyte Crystal matrices)   
* Negotiate between Divine Houses and mortals 

**Services Offered:**

| Service | Cost | Risk |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Resurrection Insurance** | **5,000 Cr/year** | **If broker fails, you experience True Death** |
| **Soul Quality Assessment** | **500 Cr** | **Reveals your soul grade (uncomfortable knowledge)** |
| **Post-Death Soul Allocation** | **1,000 Cr (one-time)** | **Your soul goes where you want, not House Mortis default** |
| **Black Market Contacts** | **Variable** | **Legal liability, Divine House attention** |
| **Echo Appeasement** | **2,000 Cr \+ ritual materials** | **Might not work if Echo is too angry** |

**Notable Soul Brokers:**

* **The Valorian Syndicate:** Largest legal operation, Spires-based   
* **Soulwright Collective:** Specializes in resurrection for the wealthy   
* **The Forgotten Market:** Black market soul trade (location changes constantly)   
* **House Mortis Direct:** Official Divine House service (most trustworthy, most expensive) 

  4. ### **The Bloom's Economic Impact**

**As the Bloom Advances:**

**Initiate Epoch:**

* Divine Crystal prices rise (scarcity as mines are abandoned)   
* Soul insurance premiums increase   
* Frontier property values plummet   
* Anchor construction business booms 

**Advanced Epoch:**

* Mass refugee crisis (entire sectors evacuated)

**Ascended Epoch:**

* Only the Godforge can restore balance (plot climax) 

  5. ### **Revelation Ladder: When to Reveal What**

**Initiate Epoch (Levels 1-10):**

* **Lore Depth:** Rumors of soul value, Freighter sightings, Divine Crystal scarcity

**Advanced Epoch (Levels 11-20):**

**Ascended Epoch (Levels 21-30):**

* **Lore Depth:** Full Soul Cycle revealed, Eternal machinery glimpsed, Godforge location discovered, Voidborn transformations understood

  6. ### **Eternal Contact Warning**

**What Happens if Someone Witnesses an Eternal:**

**Spirit Save DC 30** (nearly impossible for most mortals):

**Success:** You glimpse cosmic truth but:

* Gain 1 permanent level of Exhaustion   
* Receive Realm-specific curse (creativity drains if you witness The Architect, memories fade if you witness The Timekeeper, etc.)

* Divine Houses become aware of you 

**Failure:** Permanent madness:

* You can function but reality never quite makes sense   
* Random hallucinations of Eternal machinery   
* Temporal displacement (you answer questions before they're asked)   
* Cannot distinguish past/present/future reliably   
* Other Realms bleed into your perception (see shadows of Truth, hear whispers of Void) 

**Divine House Response:**

* Immediate recruitment attempts (you've seen too much—join or die)   
* Or immediate assassination (you're a liability)   
* Or indefinite detention (you're too valuable to kill, too dangerous to free) 

**House Founders Who Glimpse Eternals:**

* Either ascend (become something greater than mortal)   
* Or shatter (go completely insane, often becoming Voidborn)

8. # **SECTION 6: ADVANCED SYSTEMS**

Soulvegr thrives on the tension between Divine Crystal-forged realities and the Void's relentless unmaking. Section 6 equips GMs with advanced systems: Void mutations, wild magic, gear corruption, Divine Crystal crafting, and political intrigue.

1. ## **6.1 The Void Mutation System**

When characters spend time in Void-touched zones, they risk transformation. This system turns Void exposure into dramatic character evolution—sometimes beneficial, sometimes horrifying, always memorable.

1. ### **How Mutations Work**

**Tracking Void Exposure Points (VEP):**

Characters gain VEP through:

* **1 VEP per hour** in Bloom Edge zones (Spirit Save DC 20 negates)   
* **1 VEP per round** of direct Void contact WITH protection (instant death without)   
* **1 VEP** when taking 20+ damage from Void sources 

**Mutation Thresholds:**

* **3 VEP:** Roll on Minor Mutation Table (d100)   
* **6 VEP:** Roll on Major Mutation Table (d100)   
* **10 VEP:** Voidborn Transformation (special table, character still playable) 

**Protection from VEP:**

* **Void Channeling Origin:** Grants Advantage on saves   
* **Void-Touched Race:** Immune to minor mutations   
* **Aegisite (Sanctuary Crystal) Wards:** Prevent VEP gain for 1 hour (consumes shard)   
* **Certain Talents/Perks:** As specified in character options 

  2. ### **Minor Mutation Table (d100)**

*Roll when character reaches 3 Void Exposure Points*

**Physical Mutations (1-40)**

1. **Backwards Knees** \- Your knees now bend the wrong way. \+5 ft movement speed, but you walk like a creepy bird. NPCs are disturbed.   
2. **Glowing Eyes** \- Your eyes glow faintly in darkness. You have darkvision 60 ft, but can't hide in the dark (your eyes give you away).   
3. **Extra Finger** \- One hand grows a sixth finger. \+2 to Sleight of Hand checks, but gloves don't fit and people stare.   
4. **Shadow Hair** \- Your hair is now made of living shadow. It moves on its own and you can style it with your mind. \+5 to Intimidation, \-5 to looking normal.   
5. **Crystalline Skin Patches** \- Parts of your skin become translucent crystal. \+1 AC, but you sparkle in light like a discount vampire.   
6. **Elongated Tongue** \- Your tongue is now 18 inches long. Can lick things from 5 ft away (utility and weird). Disadvantage on Persuasion (you lisp).   
7. **Eyebrow Sentience** \- Your eyebrows are now independently alive and expressive. They reveal your emotions even when you lie. Advantage on Deception checks against people who find it hilarious, Disadvantage against serious folk.   
8. **Retractable Claws** \- Your fingernails become retractable claws. Unarmed strikes deal 1d6 slashing, but you keep scratching yourself by accident (1 damage per day).   
9. **Bioluminescent Veins** \- Your veins glow softly through your skin. You provide dim light in 10 ft radius. Cannot stealth effectively, but you look like a rave.   
10. **Backwards Voice** \- You speak normally but others hear it backwards. You must physically reverse your speech to be understood. Exhausting and hilarious.   
11. **Goat Eyes** \- Your pupils become rectangular like a goat's. Advantage on Perception, but deeply unsettling to everyone. Animals trust you more though (+5 Animal Handling).   
12. **Extra Joints** \- Your limbs gain an extra joint each. You can contort weirdly. Advantage on Acrobatics and escaping bonds, but armor doesn't fit right (-1 AC from poorly fitted gear).   
13. **Hovering Hair** \- Your hair floats 6 inches above your head at all times, even underwater. Looks absurd. \+5 to Intimidation against people who take you seriously, \-5 against everyone else who's trying not to laugh.   
14. **Scales in Random Patches** \- You grow fish/reptile scales in random spots. \+1 AC, but itchy (Disadvantage on Concentration checks during combat).   
15. **Metallic Teeth** \- Your teeth become various metals (gold, silver, iron). Bite attack deals 1d6 piercing. You also set off metal detectors and taste metal constantly.   
16. **Echo Touch** \- Everything you touch echoes the sound of touching it 6 seconds later. Advantage on Intimidation ("did you hear that?"), Disadvantage on Stealth (delayed sound reveals you).   
17. **Gravity-Defying Hair** \- Your hair is unaffected by gravity. It spreads out in all directions like you're underwater. Looks magnificent or ridiculous depending on viewer. Requires no styling.   
18. **Kaleidoscope Eyes** \- Your irises become prismatic. You see colors no one else can see. Advantage on Investigation (noticing details), but bright light gives you migraines.   
19. **Prehensile Toes** \- Your toes can grip like fingers. You can hold items with your feet. \+5 to Athletics (climbing), but you compulsively take off your shoes.   
20. **Antennae Sprout** \- Small antennae grow from your temples. You can sense nearby creatures (like tremorsense 10 ft), but they wiggle when you're nervous and everyone notices.   
21. **Rubber Bones** \- Your bones become flexible like rubber. You can squeeze through 6-inch gaps, but you're floppy and weird. Advantage on Acrobatics, Disadvantage on Athletics (no rigidity).   
22. **Chameleon Skin (Broken)** \- Your skin changes color... but to the WRONG colors. You turn bright pink in darkness, neon yellow in forests. Disadvantage on Stealth (you're hilariously visible).   
23. **Third Nostril** \- You grow a third nostril on your forehead. Advantage on Perception (smell), but constant sinus issues and you look bizarre.   
24. **Backwards Hands** \- Your hands are now on backwards (palms face outward). You adapt (no mechanical penalty), but it looks SO wrong and you can't shake hands normally.   
25. **Floating Ribs** \- Your ribs detach and hover around your torso. \+2 to DV (they block attacks), but doctors are horrified and you click when you move.   
26. **Extra Elbow** \- One arm gains an extra elbow joint. \+5 ft reach with that arm, but coordination is weird (Disadvantage on two-handed weapon attacks for 1 week while adapting).   
27. **Tentacle Finger** \- One of your fingers becomes a small tentacle. It's prehensile and useful, but deeply off-putting. \+2 to Sleight of Hand, \-2 to not being weird.   
28. **Phosphorescent Teeth** \- Your teeth glow in the dark. You can provide light by smiling, but you can't hide your grin (Disadvantage on Stealth if smiling).   
29. **Musical Bones** \- Your bones hum faintly when you move. You can play melodies by walking/gesturing. Advantage on Performance, Disadvantage on Stealth (you're a xylophone).   
30. **Detachable Thumb** \- One thumb can pop off and crawl around like a spider. It's still connected to your nervous system (you control it). Scouting tool, but absolutely horrifying to witness.   
31. **Crystallized Tears** \- When you cry, you produce small gemstones instead of tears. Worth 1 Credit each. You must cry weekly or suffer headaches.   
32. **Shadow Sweat** \- You sweat liquid shadow instead of water. It evaporates into darkness. You leave dark patches everywhere, but you're impossible to track by scent.   
33. **Magnetic Palms** \- Your palms attract metal objects weakly. You can catch thrown weapons (Advantage on disarm checks), but loose change sticks to you.   
34. **Prismatic Blood** \- Your blood is rainbow-colored. When bleeding, you create dim light in 5 ft radius. Easy to track but looks fabulous.   
35. **Redundant Organs** \- You grow backup organs. If you lose a kidney, you've got spares. \+10 max HP, but you're slightly bloated and heavy.   
36. **Origami Joints** \- Your joints can fold in impossible angles (like origami). You can compress yourself to fit in a 1-ft cube. Freaky as hell.   
37. **Living Tattoo** \- One of your tattoos (or a new one appears) becomes animated and mobile across your skin. It comments on things (GM voices it). Helpful or annoying depending on GM mood.   
38. **Seasonal Skin** \- Your skin tone changes with seasons. Spring \= green, Summer \= gold, Fall \= orange, Winter \= white. Advantage on Stealth in appropriate environments, obvious in others.   
39. **Telescoping Neck** \- Your neck can extend up to 5 ft. Great for looking over walls. Terrifying in practice. You make giraffe noises when extending.

  2. ## **6.2 Wild Magic and Reality Instability**

When Powers are used in Void-touched zones, reality itself becomes unpredictable. Magic doesn't just fail—it **mutates**.

1. ### **How Wild Magic Works**

**Trigger Conditions:**

Wild Magic surges occur when:

* **Using Powers in Bloom-touched zones** \- Base 10% chance per Power cast   
* **High Void Exposure** \- Additional chance \= (VEP × 5%)   
* **Void-Touched Divine Crystals** \- 20% chance when using corrupted Crystal items   
* **Reality Fractures** \- Automatic surge in areas of severe Void corruption   
* **Soul Burning nearby** \- 15% chance when Powers used within 1 hour of soul burning 

**The Surge Check:**

When triggering conditions are met:

1. Roll d100   
2. If roll is ≤ surge chance, Wild Magic activates   
3. Roll d100 on Wild Magic Surge Table   
4. Apply result immediately 

**Example:** Character with 4 VEP casts a Power in a Bloom Edge zone.

* Base chance: 10%   
* VEP bonus: 4 × 5% \= 20%   
* Total: 30% chance   
* Roll d100: If 1-30, surge occurs

**Frequency Control:**

If Wild Magic becomes too frequent or disruptive:

* **Lower base chance** to 5% instead of 10%   
* **Award Advantage on surge prevention** for good roleplay   
* **Let players spend VEP** to reduce surge chance (spend 1 VEP \= \-5% surge chance for one Power) 

**Tone Calibration:**

Mix result types based on your table's preferences:

* **Comedy-Focused:** Roll on 1-35 range more often   
* **Action-Focused:** Roll on 36-65 range more often   
* **Horror-Focused:** Roll on 66-100 range more often 

**Player Agency:**

Give players choice when surge happens:

* **Accept the surge** (roll on table)   
* **Resist the surge** (spend 5 EP, make Spirit save DC 20, success \= no surge but Power fails)   
* **Channel the surge** (spend 10 EP, pick which 1/3 of table to roll on)

* Completely transformed (looks alien, feels wrong)   
* \+3 to primary function

* User gains 1 VEP per day of use

  4. ## **6.4 Divine Crystal Crafting System**

Advanced crafting channels Substance into Crystal-forged masterpieces. This system turns crafting into dramatic moments with meaningful consequences.

1. ### **Basic Crafting Process**

**Step 1: Gather Materials**

* **Divine Crystal Shards:** 1-5 shards (depending on item complexity and whether Crystal is required or optional enhancement)   
* **Soul Energy Binding:** Soul shards for power (Ember minimum for basic items, higher grades for legendary items)   
* **Mundane Materials:** Base item components (metal, circuitry, organic materials, etc.)   
* **Facilities:** Workshop reduces DC by 5; master forge reduces by 10 

**Step 2: Select Crystal by Color Resonance**

Matching Crystal color to crafting purpose grants **\+5 to crafting check** (or **\-5 to DC**):

| Item Type | Best Crystal | Resonance Bonus |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Weapons** | **Conflictite (War \- Blood Red)** | **\+5** |
| **Armor** | **Aegisite (Sanctuary \- Silver-Blue)** | **\+5** |
| **Tools** | **Genesyte (Creation \- Emerald Green)** | **\+5** |
| **Starship Cores** | **Temporalite (Time \- Silver-Blue)** | **\+5** |
| **Medical Devices** | **Vitastone (Life \- Pulsing Crimson)** | **\+5** |
| **Information Systems** | **Veritite (Truth \- Clear/Gold)** | **\+5** |
| **Stealth Gear** | **Umbrastone (Shadow \- Black/Purple)** | **\+5** |
| **Control Systems** | **Sovereignite (Dominion \- Royal Purple)** | **\+5** |

**Mismatched Crystals: No bonus (but still works)**

**Opposing Crystals:** \+10 DC penalty if using opposite Realm pairs in same item (Life \+ Death, Creation \+ Void, Liberation \+ Dominion)

**Step 3: Crystal Purity Check**

Roll **d20 \+ Substance \+ Crafting vs. Crystal Purity DC:**

| Crystal Purity | Base DC | Power Level | Special Notes |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Flawed** | **25** | **50% effectiveness** | **15% chance of malfunction per use** |
| **Pure** | **20** | **100% effectiveness** | **Reliable, standard power** |
| **Perfect** | **15** | **150% effectiveness** | **Enhanced power, reduces crafting time by 50%** |
| **Void-Touched** | **30** | **200% effectiveness** | **Unstable; 20% catastrophic failure chance** |

**Modifiers:**

* **Color Resonance:** \-5 DC (or \+5 to roll)   
* **Master Forge:** \-10 DC   
* **Multiple Crystals:** \+5 DC per additional Crystal beyond first   
* **Soul Grade Used:** Flame+ reduces DC by 2, Blaze+ reduces by 5   
* **Assistance:** Another crafter with Crafting 5+ grants Advantage 

**Step 4: Infusion Roll & Results**

5. ## **6.5 Divine Politics and Leverage**

Divine Crystals aren't just crafting materials—they're political power, economic leverage, and casus belli all in one.

1. ### **The Two Layers of Crystal Politics**

**Divine Layer (Divine House Level):**

Divine Houses enforce Realm authority through **edicts**—cosmic law backed by Divine Crystal power.

**Example Edicts:**

| House | Edict Type | Mechanical Effect | Political Impact |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |

| **House Veritas** | **Truth Mandate** | **Lies become physically painful (1d6 psychic per lie) within designated zones** | **Controls information flow** |
| **House Libertas** | **Emancipation Order** | **All slave bonds/prisons broken in sector** | **Destabilizes slave economies** |
| **House Sanctuary** | **Protection Decree** | **Designated zone becomes sanctuary (attacks impossible)** | **Protects refugees but limits House Vortheon military** |
| **House Vortheon** | **War Declaration** | **Formal combat challenge (refusing \= cowardice, lose all allies)** | **Forces conflicts into the open** |

**Divine Crystal Wars:**

Divine Houses wage proxy wars over Crystal mine control:

* **Military Occupation:** House Vortheon specialty (Conflictite mines seized by force)   
* **Legal Maneuvering:** House Veritas specialty (Veritite-backed "proof" of ancient ownership)   
* **Shadow Operations:** House Umbra specialty (Umbrastone mines "liberated" by invisible agents)

**Mortal Layer (Player-Accessible Level):**

      4. ### **Politics Resolution Flow**

Use this structure for political encounters:

**Step 1: Present Crystal Dilemma**

Set up the situation with competing interests:

*"House Vitae claims this Vitastone grove is sacred and must be protected. House Artifex wants to mine it for bio-ship construction. Both Houses are approaching war—and your settlement is caught in the middle. What do you do?"*

**Step 2: Players Gather Crystal Leverage**

Players must earn tokens (minimum 3 for meaningful influence):

**Success Methods:**

* **Crafting:** Create impressive Crystal items (DC 20+) to prove worth   
* **Intrigue:** Gather blackmail material on House representatives (Investigation DC 25\)   
* **Combat:** Win House-sanctioned trials or duels   
* **Diplomacy:** Convince third-party Houses to support your position (Persuasion DC 22\)   
* **Economics:** Control Crystal trade routes or stockpiles 

Track tokens gained. Narrate each success:

*"Your Crystal infusion succeeds brilliantly. The Vitastone pulses with life energy, creating a medical device that even House Vitae's healers admire. House Artifex representatives take notice—you've proven you understand the craft. Gain 1 Leverage token."*

**Step 3: Contested Roll**

**Highest Authority \+ Persuasion/Leadership vs. House Proxy's Authority**

If players have Leverage tokens, they can:

* **Spend 1 token** for Advantage   
* **Spend 3 tokens** to add \+5 to roll   
* **Spend 5 tokens** to auto-succeed (within reason) 

House Proxy typically has: **Authority 18-22, Persuasion/Leadership \+8 to \+12**

**Step 4: Outcome Reshapes Reality**

**Player Victory:**

* Favorable treaty (mine access, safe passage, alliance)   
* House owes favor (future plot hook)   
* Crystal economy shifts (your faction gains power)   
* Rival Houses notice (new opportunities/threats) 

**House Victory:**

* Players must concede (but gain knowledge for next time)   
* Territory claimed by House   
* Crystal scarcity increases   
* Future conflict inevitable 

**Stalemate:**

* Temporary truce   
* Third-party arbitration required   
* Both sides seek more Leverage   
* Tension escalates

* **Lead negotiator (Kaine):** Authority 18, Persuasion \+7

* **Rolls:** 15 (with Advantage) \+ 18 \+ 7 \= 40 

**House Artifex Proxy:** Authority 20, Persuasion \+9

* **Rolls:** 12 \+ 20 \+ 9 \= 41 

**House Libertas Proxy:** Authority 19, Persuasion \+8

* **Rolls:** 8 \+ 19 \+ 8 \= 35

**Narrative:**

*House Artifex's representative—a stern woman in emerald-green robes—studies you for a long moment. "You crafted that diagnostic tool. Impressive work. Very well. We accept your proposal... for now. But understand: if production falls, or if you sell to others, this deal ends. Violently."*

1. ## **7.1 Blood Awakenings and Mentors**

As characters grow in power, they may discover hidden connections to the Divine Houses and the Realms of Influence themselves.

1. ### **Blood Awakenings**

**What They Are:**

Moments when a character discovers (or has revealed) a connection to one of the 12 Realms of Influence. This isn't necessarily genetic—it could be:

* **Ancestral:** Distant Divine House lineage   
* **Soul-Based:** Their soul resonates with a specific Realm   
* **Cosmic Accident:** Exposure to Divine Crystals awakened dormant potential   
* **Chosen:** An Eternal or Divine House marked them for a purpose 

  2. ### **When Blood Awakenings Trigger**

**Level-Based Triggers:**

* **Level 5:** First awakening (minor connection revealed)   
* **Level 10:** Second awakening (connection strengthens)   
* **Level 15:** Third awakening (full Realm affinity manifests) 

**Event-Based Triggers:**

* **Near-Death Experience:** Reduced to 0 HP by Realm-aligned damage   
* **Divine Crystal Resonance:** Successfully crafting with specific Crystal type (Critical Success)   
* **Void Exposure:** Surviving prolonged Void contact (Spirit Save Critical Success)   
* **Divine House Contact:** Direct interaction with House founder or champion   
* **Godforge Vision:** Glimpsing cosmic machinery during major ritual 

  3. ### **Determining the Realm Connection**

**Method 1: Roll Randomly (d12)**

  6. ### **Mentors: Guides to Greater Power**

Mentors are powerful beings who guide characters through Epoch transitions. They are gatekeepers—without mentor approval, advancement beyond Initiate Epoch is nearly impossible.

7. ### **Mentor Types**

**1\. Living Demigod**

**Description:** Ascended champion of a Divine House, wielding significant Realm authority

**Benefits:**

**Risks:**

* **Loyalty Oath:** You must swear service to mentor's House   
* **Divine House Vendettas:** Inherit mentor's enemies   
* **Missions:** Mentor assigns quests (refusing damages relationship)   
* **Political Entanglement:** Drawn into House wars 

**Crystal Tie:** Mentor's signature Realm Crystal (they grant you affinity with it)

**Example Mentor: Archon Velarius of House Dominion**

* Offers training in authority and command   
* Demands you enforce House edicts in Frontier zones   
* Grants access to Sovereignite mines   
* Enemies: House Libertas revolutionaries who want you dead 

**2\. Echo Ancestor**

**Description:** Ghostly remnant of a powerful soul bound to a Divine Crystal fragment

**Benefits:**

* **Dream Guidance:** Receive visions and warnings while sleeping   
* **Crystal Lore:** Deep knowledge of Divine Crystal history and locations   
* **No Political Ties:** Echo has no living allies or enemies   
* **Flexible Training:** Learn at your own pace 

**Risks:**

* **Old Grudges:** Echo's ancient enemies might still exist (immortal Divine House members)   
* **Shard Hauntings:** Echo is bound to specific Crystal—you must protect it   
* **Obsession:** Echo may push you toward goals that died with them   
* **Unstable:** Echo could fade or become corrupted 

**Crystal Tie:** Echo is bound to a specific Crystal shard (often their bloodline's Realm)

**Example Mentor: The Whisper of Chronolite**

* Ancient warrior bound to an Eternity Crystal shard   
* Teaches endurance and oath-binding   
* Warns of coming Bloom expansion via prophetic dreams   
* Enemies: Long-dead, but their descendants remember and hate your lineage

**Description:** Living embodiment of a Divine Crystal fragment—a being made of crystallized Realm energy

**Benefits:**

* **Instant Power:** Immediately grants one Advanced Power (Tier 2\) at no cost   
* **Crystal Resonance:** Perfect affinity with mentor's Realm Crystal (auto-success on basic crafting)   
* **Realm Insight:** Deep understanding of your Realm's cosmic role 

**Risks:**

* **Possession Hazard:** Soul-Avatar may attempt to possess you (Spirit Save DC 25 to resist)

**Crystal Tie:** Avatar IS a fragment of the Realm's Divine Crystal (literally made of Chronolite, Veritite, etc.)

**Example Mentor: The Genesyte Shard-Mind**

* Sentient fragment of Creation Realm Crystal   
* Grants instant mastery of matter manipulation   
* Wants to become "more real" by merging with your soul   
* Constantly attracting Voidborn who want to consume it

* Grants knowledge of Divine House politics   
* Prepares student for greater cosmic role 

**Step 5: Release or Betrayal**

* **Releases student:** You've graduated, go forth   
* **Demands greater service:** Join House permanently   
* **Betrays student:** Attempt to steal power/harvest soul

  2. ## **7.2 Epoch Transitions: Initiate to Ascended**

Epoch transitions are not automatic at specific levels—they require narrative milestones and cosmic investment.

1. ### **Initiate to Advanced Epoch (Level 11\)**

**Requirements (Choose ONE):**

1. **Mentor Bond:** Complete mentor's quest chain and gain their blessing   
2. **Blood Awakening:** Experience full Realm awakening (all 3 awakening stages)   
3. **Soul Sacrifice Ritual:** Burn 50 Spark-grade souls in Vitastone (Life Crystal) ritual (morally fraught) 

**Mechanical Rewards:**

* **\+20 AP** immediately   
* **Attribute Cap → 25** (was 20\)   
* **Advanced Powers Unlock:** Can now purchase Tier 2 Powers   
* **First Crystal Affinity:** Choose one Divine Crystal type—reduce crafting DC by 5 

**Narrative Moment:**

*"The Vitastone grove pulses with emerald light as you complete the ritual. Your mentor's voice echoes: 'Mortal limits shatter tonight. Claim your Crystal birthright.' Divine energy floods your soul—you are no longer merely mortal. The Divine Houses now see you as a piece on their cosmic board."*

**Consequences:**

* Divine Houses take direct interest (recruitment or elimination)   
* Soul grade increases (Spark → Flame minimum)   
* Reality treats you differently (minor physics exemptions)   
* Void notices you (Bloom attracted to significant souls) 

  2. ### **Advanced to Ascended Epoch (Level 21\)**

**Requirements (ALL THREE):**

1. **Mentor Completion:** Fulfill all mentor obligations OR surpass mentor (prove superior)   
2. **Crystal Pyramid Challenge:** Construct and attune a 12-shard Divine Crystal matrix   
3. **Anchor Ritual:** Burn 100 Flame-grade souls OR stabilize major Anchor against Void incursion 

**Mechanical Rewards:**

* **\+30 AP** immediately   
* **Attribute Cap → 30** (was 25\)   
* **Elite Crystal Matrices:** Can craft 12-Crystal ship cores and reality anchors   
* **Cosmic Significance:** Divine Houses treat you as near-peer   
* **Voidborn Resistance:** If you have VEP, gain \+5 to all saves vs. further Void exposure 

**Narrative Moment:**

*"The 12 Divine Crystals align in perfect opposition—Eternity facing Time, Creation opposing Void, Liberation balancing Dominion. As the final Flame soul ignites the matrix, reality bends. You are no longer simply powerful—you are significant. The Eternals themselves take notice. The Godforge whispers your name."*

**Consequences:**

* Can challenge Divine House champions   
* Voidborn actively hunt you (significant souls attract them)   
* Bloom expansion affects your choices (you can stabilize reality)   
* Godforge access becomes possible   
* Other Ascended beings see you as rival or ally   
* Mutations stabilize (if Voidborn, you've fully adapted) 

  3. ### **Transition Ritual Mechanics**

**Group Ceremony at Crystal Anchor:**

All party members gather at functioning Divine Crystal Anchor (or construct one for this purpose).

**Ritual Requirements:**

* **Divine Crystals:** 12 shards arranged in opposition pairs   
* **Soul Energy:** Required soul sacrifice (50 Spark for Advanced, 100 Flame for Ascended)   
* **Time:** 8-hour uninterrupted ceremony   
* **Location:** Must be at stable reality (not Void-touched) 

**Ritual Check:**

Each participant rolls: **Spirit Save DC 25 \+ Crystal purity bonus**

**Crystal Purity Bonus:**

* Perfect Crystals: \-5 DC (easier)   
* Pure Crystals: \+0 DC (standard)   
* Flawed Crystals: \+5 DC (harder)   
* Void-Touched: \+10 DC (dangerous) 

**Results:**

| Outcome | Effect |
| ----- | ----- |
| **All Succeed** | **Perfect transition, no complications, bonus \+10 AP to all** |
| **Majority Succeed** | **Standard transition, those who failed gain Soul-Scar Hindrance** |
| **Majority Fail** | **Crystal backlash: 5d10 force damage, all Crystals shatter, no transition** |
| **All Fail** | **Catastrophic failure: Void rift opens, Eternal judgment triggered, campaign-altering event** |

   3. ## **7.3 Endgame Arcs and Godforge Claims**

Ascended campaigns culminate in cosmic-scale conflicts where players' choices reshape reality itself.

1. ### **Endgame Campaign Frameworks**

Choose one primary arc or blend multiple:

2. ### **Arc 1: Crystal Cartel Empire**

**Goal:** Monopolize the Divine Crystal economy and build a Godforge-access fleet

**Structure:**

* **Levels 21-25:** Secure Crystal mines across multiple sectors   
* **Levels 26-28:** Construct 12-Crystal Matrix capital ships   
* **Levels 29-30:** Challenge Divine Houses for Godforge access 

**Key Encounters:**

* Divine House wars over mine rights   
* Rival cartels (other Ascended NPCs with same goal)   
* Void incursions attracted by Crystal concentration   
* Voidborn assaults (mutations spreading among workers)   
* Godforge guardians testing worthiness 

**Victory Condition:**

* Control 6+ Crystal mine types   
* Possess 3+ complete 12-Crystal Matrix ships   
* Prove balanced authority across all 12 Realms 

  3. ### **Arc 2: Soul Cycle Revolution**

**Goal:** End Divine House Mortis's Tulip Freighter monopoly and redirect souls to player-controlled Pantheon

**Structure:**

* **Levels 21-25:** Raid Tulip Freighters, expose soul-trafficking   
* **Levels 26-28:** Establish independent Soulvegr gates   
* **Levels 29-30:** Form new Pantheon or overthrow existing one 

**Key Encounters:**

* Tulip Freighter assaults (heavily defended)   
* Divine House Mortis retaliation (Reapers, soul-hunters)   
* Echo swarms from disrupted Soul Cycle   
* Ethical dilemmas (soul burning temptations vs. revolutionary ideals) 

**Victory Condition:**

* Redirect 30%+ of sector's soul traffic   
* Establish 3+ independent Soulvegr gates   
* Gain support of 6+ Divine Houses (form new Pantheon) 

  4. ### **Arc 3: Anchor War**

**Goal:** Race rival Divine Houses to stabilize collapsing reality against the Bloom

**Structure:**

* **Levels 21-25:** Defend existing Anchors, construct new ones   
* **Levels 26-28:** Reclaim Void-touched territories   
* **Levels 29-30:** Final stand at Bloom's edge OR allow controlled collapse 

**Key Encounters:**

* Voidborn hordes (Amalgams, Nullifiers, Unremembered)   
* Rival Houses sabotaging your Anchors (to make you fail so they look better)   
* Moral choices (which territories to save, which to abandon)   
* Mutated populations (Voidborn refugees seeking sanctuary)   
* Godforge pilgrimage to request Eternal intervention 

**Victory Condition:**

* Stabilize 10+ sectors with functioning Anchors   
* Reverse Bloom expansion in at least 1 major region   
* Gain Eternal acknowledgment (proof you're worthy of cosmic authority) 

  5. ### **Throne Claim: The Godforge Endgame**

The ultimate Ascended campaign conclusion: accessing the Godforge and claiming Realm authority.

6. ### **Throne Claim Flow**

**Stage 1: Collect Throne Fragments (12 Total)**

One fragment per Realm, scattered across the multiverse:

| Realm | Fragment Location | Guardian/Challenge |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Eternity** | **Eternal Archive vault** | **Time-locked, requires perfect patience (wait 100 years OR solve temporal puzzle)** |
| **Truth** | **Library of All Lies** | **Navigate maze where every sign is false** |
| **Shadow** | **Shrouded Depths** | **Find fragment in absolute darkness (cannot use light)** |
| **Creation** | **Star nursery core** | **Survive reality-forging heat and pressure** |
| **Life** | **Grove heart-tree root** | **Prove you cherish life (pacifist challenge)** |
| **Time** | **Causality loop station** | **Escape time loop while maintaining sanity** |
| **Sanctuary** | **Perfect sanctuary planet** | **Convince inhabitants you deserve sacred relic** |
| **War** | **Eternal Battleground** | **Defeat legendary War-Champion in single combat** |
| **Void** | **Bloom's edge station** | **Survive prolonged Void exposure to reach fragment (mutation risk)** |
| **Death** | **Soulvegr gate nexus** | **Navigate realm of the dead without dying** |
| **Liberation** | **Free Chains fleet** | **Prove you've broken more chains than you've forged** |
| **Dominion** | **Throne world palace** | **Demonstrate absolute authority (command test)** |

**Each fragment quest is a 2-3 session arc.**

**Stage 2: Assemble the Godforge Key**

**Requirements:**

* All 12 Throne Fragments   
* 12-Crystal Matrix (one Pure-quality shard per Realm)   
* Proof of Balance (demonstrate mastery of opposing Realms)   
* **Location:** Galaxy core, hidden dimension accessible only with complete Key 

**Assembly Challenge:**

Arrange Crystals and Fragments in sacred pattern:

* **DC 35 Substance \+ Engineering check**   
* **Failure:** Reality backlash (5d10 force damage, Void rift opens)   
* **Success:** Godforge portal opens 

**Stage 3: Realm Authority Contests**

Once inside the Godforge, you must prove worthy of each Realm:

**For Each Realm:**

**Roll:** d20 \+ Highest Relevant Attribute \+ Crystal Purity Bonus vs. **DC 35**

**Modifiers:**

* Perfect Crystal: \+5   
* Pure Crystal: \+0   
* Flawed Crystal: \-5   
* Channeling Origin of that Realm: \+5   
* Opposing Realm mastery: \+3 (e.g., mastering Liberation helps with Dominion contest)   
* **Voidborn Status:** \+3 to Void Realm contest only 

**Results:**

* **Success:** Claim that Realm's authority (can wield its cosmic power)   
* **Failure:** Realm rejects you (cannot use its authority) 

**Stage 4: Eternal Verdict**

The Twelve Eternals judge your claim:

**Criteria:**

* **Balance:** Did you claim opposing Realms equally? (Eternity/Time, Life/Death, etc.)   
* **Worthiness:** Have your actions served the Cosmic Design?   
* **Intent:** Do you seek to maintain balance or shatter it? 

**Imbalanced Claims Trigger Void Cascade:**

* Claim Creation without Void → Bloom accelerates catastrophically   
* Claim Dominion without Liberation → Reality becomes tyrannical prison   
* Claim Life without Death → Immortal overpopulation collapses ecosystems 

  7. ### **Victory Conditions**

**7+ Realms Claimed (Balanced):**

* **New Eternal Design Era**   
* Players become Divine House founders   
* Reshape cosmic law   
* End the Bloom (or channel it constructively)   
* **Campaign epilogue:** 100 years later, see results of your rule 

**4-6 Realms Claimed:**

* **Join Existing Pantheon as Council**   
* Significant influence, not dominion   
* Share power with existing Divine Houses   
* **Campaign epilogue:** Political maneuvering for greater authority 

**\<4 Realms Claimed:**

* **Powerful Retainers**   
* Serve Divine Houses, but with respect   
* Legendary status, but not cosmic authority   
* **Campaign epilogue:** Adventures as House Champions 

**Total Failure (Imbalanced or \<3 Realms):**

* **Defeat and Reincarnation**   
* Souls reincarnate as Initiate Epoch mortals   
* Crystal memories intact but hazy   
* **Campaign epilogue:** Haunting visions of "another life" drive new adventures 

  8. ### **Crystal Apocalypse Scale**

Partial victories with unbalanced Realm claims cause cascading cosmic failures:

**Example Imbalances:**

**Sovereignite (Dominion) without Liberite (Liberation):**

* Tyranny spreads unchecked   
* All governments become authoritarian   
* Rebellions impossible (magically suppressed)   
* Freedom itself becomes illegal 

**Vitastone (Life) without Mortisyte (Death):**

* Nothing can die   
* Populations explode   
* Resources collapse   
* Suffering becomes eternal 

**Genesyte (Creation) without Nihilite (Void):**

* Uncontrolled matter generation   
* Reality overflows with "stuff"   
* Bloom expands massively to compensate   
* Everything drowns in infinite creation 

**Nihilite (Void) dominance without Genesyte (Creation):**

* Bloom consumes everything   
* Nothing new can be made   
* Reality slowly unmade   
* Eventual total entropy 

  9. ### **The Choice: Stabilize or Shatter**

Players at this level can:

**Option 1: Maintain the Eternal Design**

* Accept role as cosmic maintenance workers   
* Preserve balance as Eternals intended   
* Stable but static universe   
* *"Good" ending for those who value order* 

**Option 2: Shatter the Design**

* Reject Eternal authority   
* Attempt new cosmic system   
* Chaotic but potentially better   
* *"Freedom" ending for revolutionaries* 

**Option 3: Hybrid Approach**

* Maintain balance but reform system   
* Reduce Divine House tyranny while preserving stability   
* Difficult but achievable with right Realm mix   
* *"Best" ending (hardest to achieve)* 

  10. ### **Campaign Epilogue Scenes**

**100 Years Later (if players succeeded):**

Describe the multiverse shaped by their choices:

* Which Divine Houses endured, which fell   
* How the Bloom was handled (reversed, contained, or harnessed)   
* Soul Cycle reforms (if any)   
* Mutation acceptance or rejection in society   
* New legendary heroes following in their footsteps   
* Monuments and songs commemorating their deeds   
* **Voidborn populations** (if players embraced mutations) as new societies 

**Reincarnation (if players failed):**

Describe fleeting memories of cosmic significance:

* Dreams of Divine Crystals   
* Unexplained skills (Channeling they shouldn't know)   
* Feeling of destiny unfulfilled   
* Attraction to Realm-aligned locations   
* **Physical quirks** (minor mutations carried through reincarnation)   
* Setup for next campaign (as reincarnated versions) 

**These endgame arcs reward long-term Divine Crystal hoarding, carefully considered soul burning sacrifices, heroic Void resistance, and acceptance (or rejection) of transformation. Players transform from scavengers to cosmic architects who either stabilize or shatter the Eternal Design—and live with the consequences forever.**

5. ## **APPENDIX E: ADVENTURE AND CAMPAIGN TEMPLATES**

   1. ### **Crystal Heist One-Shot (3-Hour Session)**

**Setup:** House Umbra traitor offers coordinates to rival House Artifex's secret Genesyte (Creation Crystal) mine—but they're lying.

**Act 1: The Hook (30 minutes)**

* Traitor contacts party in shadowy bar   
* Offers 10,000 Credits \+ 1 Genesyte shard for simple retrieval job   
* Shows "proof": mine schematics, guard schedules (all fake)   
* **Complication:** Traitor is testing party for different job OR setting them up to take fall 

**Act 2: The Heist (90 minutes)**

* **Crystal Chamber:** Mine is real but Void-contaminated (DC 23 Substance to purify without triggering backlash)   
* **Security:** House Artifex Warden \+ 4 guards (see Appendix B stats)   
* **Reinforcements:** If alarm triggered, \+2 guards every 2 rounds   
* **Environmental Hazard:** Unstable Crystal vein (rolls d6 each round: on 1, pulse deals 3d6 force to all in 20 ft) 

**Act 3: The Twist (60 minutes)**

* **Extraction:** Warden boss fight \+ Echo reinforcements (from prior soul burning in mine)   
* **Moral Choice:** Burn a Genesyte shard for emergency escape power (+10 AP now, Echo consequence later) OR fight through honorably   
* **Aftermath:** If escaped, traitor either pays up OR betrays party to both Houses (now hunted by two factions) 

**Rewards:** 2-3 Genesyte shards (Pure or Flawed), 5,000-10,000 Credits, Divine House attention (positive or negative)

2. ### **Soul Cycle Campaign Arc (12 Sessions)**

**Arc Theme:** From Ember trading to Flame crisis, exploring soul economy ethics

**Sessions 1-4: Ember Economy (Initiate Epoch)**

* **Session 1:** Hired by Soul Broker to transport "package" (Ember soul shards)   
* **Session 2:** Discover soul trafficking ring, moral choice (expose or profit)   
* **Session 3:** Tulip Freighter spotted, investigate or raid   
* **Session 4:** First Echo encounter (minor haunt from someone else's burning) 

**Sessions 5-8: Spark Temptation (Advanced Epoch)**

* **Session 5:** NPC ally offers soul burning knowledge (power temptation)   
* **Session 6:** Divine House Mortis investigates party (suspicion of involvement)   
* **Session 7:** Major combat—burn Spark soul for victory OR honorable defeat?   
* **Session 8:** Echo swarm attacks (consequence of earlier burning) 

**Sessions 9-12: Flame Crisis (Ascended Epoch)**

* **Session 9:** Flame-grade soul auction, rival bidders, House politics   
* **Session 10:** If party burned souls: massive Echo army manifests   
* **Session 11:** Anchor defense—use Flame souls to stabilize OR let territory fall   
* **Session 12:** Final choice—reform Soul Cycle OR maintain status quo for power 

**Campaign Conclusion:**

* **Reformed Cycle:** Harder path, but prevents Bloom acceleration   
* **Maintained Status Quo:** Easier power gains, but Void expansion accelerates   
* **Total Burning:** Ultimate power now, but Echo apocalypse and Void cascade 

  3. ### **Void Anchor Defense (Session Template)**

**Setup:** Reality Anchor protecting key settlement is failing. Party must defend/repair.

**Prep Phase (30 minutes)**

* **Crystal Matrix Construction:** Substance \+ Engineering checks DC 25-35   
* **Gather Resources:** Need 3-6 Divine Crystal shards (various types)   
* **Fortify Position:** Set traps, position defenders, evacuate civilians 

**Incursion Wave 1: Voidborn Scouts (45 minutes)**

* 2d6 Void Wraiths probe defenses   
* Test party capabilities   
* Identify weak points in Anchor 

**Incursion Wave 2: Echo Horde (45 minutes)**

* 4d6 Echoes \+ 1d4 Soul Harvesters   
* Result of prior soul burning in sector   
* Attack Crystal matrix directly 

**Incursion Wave 3: Void Horror (60 minutes)**

* Major threat (Budget 150, see Appendix B for Void Crystal Golem as template)   
* Boss fight while maintaining Anchor   
* **Environmental hazard:** Reality fracturing (gravity shifts, time loops)   
* **Mutation risk:** All combatants gain 1 VEP per hour in zone 

**Resolution:**

* **Success:** Anchor stabilized, territory saved, Divine Houses offer alliance   
* **Partial:** Anchor holds but Crystals consumed (must find replacements), some defenders mutated   
* **Failure:** Anchor collapses, territory becomes Void-touched (permanent Bloom expansion), survivors transformed into Voidborn 

**Rewards:** Survivors' gratitude, Divine House favor, 1d4 Divine Crystal shards recovered from battlefield, potential Blood Awakening moment for PCs, mutated allies seeking cure

* Any Voidborn transformations approaching?

☐ **Next Eternal Omen Prepared**
