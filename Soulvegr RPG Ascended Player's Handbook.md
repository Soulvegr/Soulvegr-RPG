3. # **SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION**

   1. ## **1.1 Welcome to the Ascended Epoch**

Congratulations—you've conquered the Advanced Epoch. Your characters are no longer system-shaping legends negotiating with Divine House Champions. **Welcome to the Ascended Epoch (Levels 21–30)**, where you become cosmic sovereigns who:

* **Command pantheons** and forge divine hierarchies   
* **Reshape pocket realms** and alter planetary ecosystems   
* **Sit at eternal councils** with Divine House Patriarchs   
* **Navigate Bloom Edge politics** and witness reality's unmaking   
* **Attempt Godforge access** and learn the Eternals' deepest secrets   
* **Challenge the Twelve Eternals** themselves for cosmic authority 

  1. ### **This Handbook Contains Everything New for Ascended Play:**

✓ **10 new Ascended Powers per Channeling Origin** (130 total), each with the familiar 3-tier progression  
✓ **Expanded skill caps to Rank 30** (divine mastery)  
✓ **Divine Talents** (GM-awarded legendary abilities)  
✓ **Revised AP costs:** 15 AP per level with \+30 AP breakthrough bonus at Level 21  
✓ **Stat cap rises to 30**—the mortal maximum before true godhood

2. ### **Your Character's Transformation:**

**Combat Evolution:**

* Battles shatter continents and span multiple planes   
* Single Powers can destroy fleets or reshape geography   
* Divine House Champions become worthy opponents (not guaranteed victories)   
* Victory conditions expand beyond "kill enemies" to "reshape cosmic balance" 

**Social Stakes:**

* Negotiate with Divine House Patriarchs (the actual Eternals' representatives)   
* Forge alliances between multiple Divine Houses   
* Influence Crystal Leverage economy at system-wide scale   
* Your reputation affects entire Divine House policies 

**Cosmic Revelations:**

* **The Eternal Thrones:** Where the Twelve Eternals truly reside   
* **The Void's heart:** What lies at the center of the Bloom   
* **Soul creation:** How souls are actually made (not just recycled)   
* **The Godforge:** Its true purpose and how to access it 

  2. ## **1.2 What Changes in Ascended Play**

  2. ### **Playstyle Evolution:**

**Advanced Epoch:**

* System-spanning adventures   
* Faction leadership   
* Divine House diplomacy   
* Crystal acquisition   
* Soul economy temptation 

**Ascended Epoch:**

* **Cosmic conquest** (claiming star systems, establishing Divine authority)   
* **Pantheon building** (recruiting Divine allies, forging cosmic pacts)   
* **Eternal politics** (negotiating with the Twelve Eternals' representatives)   
* **Reality reshaping** (altering planar connections, stabilizing/destabilizing Bloom Edges)   
* **Godforge attempts** (seeking the mythical realm-engine that could rewrite reality) 

  3. ### **Combat Differences:**

**Advanced:** Battles last 5-10 rounds, battlefield-scale effects, multiple high-threat enemies

**Ascended:**

* Battles last 10-20+ rounds or resolve in single apocalyptic exchanges   
* **Planet-scale destruction** (Powers that reshape continents, boil oceans, shatter moons)   
* **Plane-spanning warfare** (fighting across multiple dimensions simultaneously)   
* **Divine House armies clash** (you command thousands, face equal forces)   
* **Victory \= narrative outcomes** (do you spare the House Champion? Claim the Crystal mine? Reshape local reality?) 

  4. ### **Social Encounters:**

**Advanced:** War council negotiations, Divine House diplomacy, Crystal trades, mentor obligations

**Ascended:**

* **Eternal Councils** (the Twelve Eternals judge your actions, issue cosmic edicts)   
* **Divine House Patriarch negotiations** (forge alliances between Houses, mediate territorial disputes at highest level)   
* **Soul economy mastery** (legal establishment of soul contracts, challenge House Mortis monopoly)   
* **Bloom stabilization politics** (Houses pay you to prevent reality collapse, rivals sabotage your efforts)   
* **Godforge access requests** (every cosmic power wants in—who do you trust?) 

  5. ### **Narrative Tone:**

The universe revolves around you:

* **The Eternals test you directly** (not through proxies—actual divine intervention)   
* **Your choices birth or destroy paradigms** (your actions create new Divine Houses or collapse existing ones)   
* **Reality bends to your edicts** (issue commands that alter physics, time, causality)   
* **Godhood beckons** (Level 30 is the threshold—what lies beyond?) 

**The Bloom responds to your presence.** When you use Ascended Powers, reality itself remembers. The Void grows or shrinks based on your cosmic balance.

**Divine Houses must negotiate with you as equals.** You're no longer a valuable asset—you're a sovereign power they cannot ignore.

4. # **SECTION 2: EPOCH TRANSITION**

Entering the Ascended Epoch transforms your character from system-shaping legend to cosmic sovereign. Section 2 covers the mechanical requirements, revised AP economy, and narrative milestones that mark this divine ascension.

1. ## **2.1 Requirements and Rituals**

   1. ### **Transition Trigger:**

**Level 21** (group reaches 20 Session Score points after Level 20\)

To unlock the Ascended Epoch, you must complete **ONE of these paths** (GM approves narrative fulfillment):

2. ### **Path 1: Divine Paragon Trial**

**Survive and triumph in trials set by Divine House Champions or Patriarch proxies:**

**Trial Examples:**

**House Vortheon (War) Trial:**

* Duel three Divine House Champions simultaneously in the War Arena   
* Survive 100 rounds of escalating combat challenges   
* Command mock armies in tactical warfare simulation   
* **Reward:** Recognition as War Paragon, access to House Vortheon's Conflictite mines 

**House Veritas (Truth) Trial:**

* Answer the Unanswerable Question (philosophical riddle with no correct answer—only worthy responses)   
* Reveal a cosmic secret the House doesn't know   
* Survive mental interrogation by Truth Patriarch's proxy   
* **Reward:** Truth Paragon status, ability to issue one Truth Edict per arc 

**House Nullis (Void) Trial:**

* Survive at Bloom's Edge for 7 days without protection   
* Harvest Void-Touched Crystal without mutation   
* Embrace entropy and return unchanged   
* **Reward:** Void Paragon, immunity to first 10 VEP, Voidborn respect you 

**Multiple Houses:**

* Complete trials from 2-3 different Houses   
* Prove mastery across multiple Realms   
* **Warning:** Houses may compete for your loyalty—political complications arise 

  3. ### **Path 2: Pantheon Ascension**

**Forge binding oaths with 3+ powerful mentors/Divine House members:**

**Requirements:**

* Must have at least 3 mentors/patrons from your Advanced Epoch journey   
* Each must represent different Realm (cannot all be from House Vortheon, for example)   
* Formalize cosmic pacts with eternal obligations 

**Pantheon Types:**

**Balanced Pantheon** (3+ Houses, different Realms):

* **Benefit:** Access to multiple Crystal types, political neutrality, diverse power base   
* **Cost:** Competing obligations—Houses may demand opposing actions   
* **Example:** Oath to House Vortheon (War), House Vitae (Life), House Chronos (Time) 

**Unified Pantheon** (3+ members, same House):

* **Benefit:** Deep House loyalty, rapid advancement within House hierarchy, Crystal mine ownership   
* **Cost:** House enemies become your enemies, rival Houses distrust you, bound to House politics   
* **Example:** Three House Eternal Champions bind you to Eternity Realm service 

**Rebel Pantheon** (3+ outcasts, no House affiliation):

* **Benefit:** Complete independence, forge new cosmic paradigm, challenge existing order   
* **Cost:** All Houses view you suspiciously, no Crystal mine access, must survive without institutional support   
* **Example:** Rogue Echo Ancestors, exiled Champions, Tulip Freighter lords 

**Binding Ritual:**

* Takes 24 hours at cosmic nexus (Soulvegr gate hub, Divine Crystal megastructure, Bloom Edge stabilization point)   
* Each mentor/patron grants specific boon (Power, stat boost, cosmic authority)   
* Oaths are **eternal**—breaking them has severe consequences (loss of powers, cosmic exile, soul damage) 

  4. ### **Path 3: Cosmic Soul Ritual**

**Purify and sacrifice soul energy in a dangerous sanctum ritual:**

**Requirements:**

* Sacrifice **100 Flame-grade souls** (or 10 Blaze-grade, or 1 Inferno-grade)   
* Requires access to sanctum (Divine Crystal cathedral, ancient Throne fragment, stabilized Bloom pocket)   
* Takes 7 days uninterrupted ritual 

**Risks:**

**Echo Manifestation (Guaranteed):**

* 4d6 Echoes appear within 1d4 hours   
* CR 10+ hostile spirits hunting you   
* Must defeat or appease before ritual completion 

**Divine House Mortis Response:**

* Soul burning on this scale **guarantees** House Mortis enforcer dispatch   
* Reapers arrive within 1d4 days   
* Must negotiate, flee, or fight 

**Void Expansion:**

* Local Bloom advances 50 miles   
* Reality Anchor weakens (Vitastone mine endangered)   
* Nearby settlements evacuated   
* Divine Houses investigate—you're blamed 

**Cosmic Predator Attraction:**

* Burning this much soul energy attracts **Voidborn Amalgams** (CR 15+)   
* Ancient entities that feed on soul fire   
* Arrive during ritual vulnerability 

**Why Choose This Path:**

* **No mentor obligations** (complete independence)   
* **No pantheon politics** (avoid House entanglements)   
* **Immediate, guaranteed power** (no trial failure risk)   
* **But at terrible cost**—you start Ascended Epoch with powerful enemies 

  5. ### **Immediate Mechanical Rewards (All Paths):**

Upon completing your chosen path:

✓ **Stat cap rises to 30** (from 25\)  
✓ **Skill cap rises to Rank 30** (from 20\)  
✓ **\+30 one-time AP bonus**  
✓ **Access to Ascended Powers** (can now purchase Tier 1 Ascended Powers from any Channeling Origin)  
✓ **Access to Divine Talents** (GM-awarded legendary abilities)  
✓ **Soul grade increases to Flame minimum** (Spark → Flame, Flame → Blaze if already Flame)  
✓ **Divine House recognition** (you're now cosmic-tier, Houses negotiate as equals)

6. ### **Ascension Ritual (Narrative Milestone)**

**No roll required—pure narrative moment.**

**Step 1: Choose a Cosmic Nexus**

Hold your ceremony at a location of supreme cosmic significance:

* **Divine House Sanctum** (House Eternal's Chronolite Cathedral, House Artifex's Forge-World)   
* **Ancient Throne Room** (abandoned Eternal throne, derelict Godforge fragment)   
* **Soulvegr Gate Hub** (where multiple gates converge, reality is thin)   
* **Bloom Edge Stabilization Point** (where reality fights entropy—dangerous but powerful)   
* **12-Crystal Matrix** (rare convergence of all twelve Divine Crystal types) 

**Step 2: Proclaim Your Divine Dominion**

Speak your cosmic purpose—this defines your Ascended legend:

Examples:

* *"I claim mastery of War and Eternity—endless conflict forges eternal strength"*   
* *"I embrace Shadow and Liberation—freedom thrives in hidden places"*   
* *"I defy the Bloom itself—reality shall endure through my will alone"*   
* *"I challenge the Eternals' monopoly—new gods shall rise"* 

**Your proclamation echoes across planes.** Divine Houses hear. Eternals notice. The Void responds.

**Step 3: GM Narrates Reality-Warping Acknowledgment**

The cosmos recognizes your ascension:

**Celestial Signs:**

* Stars realign into your chosen Realm symbols   
* Constellations shift permanently   
* Astrologers across systems witness impossible configurations 

**Divine House Response:**

* Proxies arrive immediately (offers, threats, marriage proposals, assassination attempts)   
* House Patriarchs send direct communication (first time most mortals receive this honor)   
* Crystal Leverage tokens offered in unprecedented quantities 

**Cosmic Forces Stir:**

* **Bloom pulses** (advances or recedes based on your nature—entropy vs. order)   
* **Reality flickers** (local physics briefly obey your will)   
* **Soul whispers** (nearby Echoes bow, flee, or challenge you)   
* **Divine Crystals resonate** (all twelve types glow in sequence, acknowledging new cosmic power) 

**Eternal Notice:**

* One of the Twelve Eternals **may** manifest directly (GM discretion)   
* Usually brief—a single word, gesture, or prophetic vision   
* This moment defines your relationship with the Eternal hierarchy 

**This is the moment legends become gods-in-waiting.**

5. # **SECTION 3: EXPANDED SKILLS**

Ascended Epoch characters achieve transcendent proficiency that rivals the Divine Houses themselves. Section 3 covers the expanded skill caps and how higher ranks reshape reality at Levels 21–30.

1. ## **3.1 Higher Skill Caps**

**Ascended Epoch Skill Cap:** Ranks 21–30 (raised from Advanced cap of 20\)

1. ### **Progressive AP Costs:**

| Rank Range | AP Cost per Rank |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Ranks 21–25** | **8 AP per rank** |
| **Ranks 26–30** | **15 AP per rank** |

**No additional costs, materials, or requirements—just pure transcendent investment through cosmic training and experience.**

2. ### **What Higher Ranks Represent:**

**Ranks 21-23:** **Transcendent Expert**  
You surpass mortal limitations. Techniques that should be impossible become routine. Divine House scouts take notice.

**Ranks 24-26:** **Divine Master**  
You rival Divine House Champions. Among the greatest practitioners in multiple star systems. Your techniques are studied, feared, copied.

**Ranks 27-29:** **Cosmic Grandmaster**  
You approach Eternal-level mastery. There are perhaps a dozen beings in the entire cosmos at your level. Reality bends to accommodate your expertise.

3. ## **4.3 Power Mechanics**

   1. ### **Casting Powers:**

**Action Economy:** Most powers require **1 Action** unless specified otherwise (Bonus Action, Reaction, etc.)

**Casting Check:** Roll **d20 \+ Relevant Attribute \+ Skill Rank**

**Target Number:**

* **Contested:** vs. target's relevant defense (e.g., your Mind \+ Mysticism vs. their Spirit \+ Resistance)   
* **Set DC:** GM determines based on cosmic difficulty (DC 35-45 typical for Ascended Powers) 

**Save DC (when power forces a save):** **8 \+ Proficiency Bonus \+ Relevant Attribute Modifier**

**At Ascended level, typical Save DCs are 25-30.**

2. ### **EP Costs:**

Ascended Powers cost significantly more EP than Advanced Powers:

| Tier | Typical EP Cost |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Tier 1** | **7–10 EP** |
| **Tier 2** | **10–15 EP** |
| **Tier 3** | **15–25 EP** |

**Specific costs are listed with each power.**

3. ### **Range and Duration:**

**Range Categories:**

* **Self:** Affects only you   
* **Touch:** Must touch the target   
* **Close (60-100 ft):** Immediate area   
* **Long (300+ ft):** Extended battlefield   
* **Planetary:** Affects entire continents or worlds   
* **System-Wide:** Affects multiple planets   
* **Cosmic:** Spans dimensions or planes 

**Duration Categories:**

* **Instant:** Effect happens immediately   
* **Rounds:** Lasts X combat rounds (6 seconds each)   
* **Scene:** Lasts 10 minutes to 1 hour (GM discretion)   
* **Days:** Extended effects (24 hours to 1 week)   
* **Permanent:** Effect lasts until dispelled or broken   
* **Reality-Altering:** Changes persist across timelines 

  4. ### **Concentration:**

Some Ascended Powers require **Concentration** to maintain:

* You can only concentrate on **one power at a time**   
* Taking damage requires a **Vitality save** (DC 10 or half damage taken, whichever is higher)   
* Failing the save ends concentration   
* You can voluntarily end concentration at any time (no action required) 

Powers requiring Concentration are marked with **(C)** in their description.
