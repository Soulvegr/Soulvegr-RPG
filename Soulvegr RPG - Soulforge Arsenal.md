3. # **1.1: THE SOUL LINK DEVICE (SLD)**

*The Hardware of Consciousness*

The SLD is the "Key" to the multiverse. It is a specialized data-housing unit that captures the frequency of a soul and translates it into digital or biological commands. Without an SLD, a player is just a biological "Ghost" unable to interact with the high-tech reality of the Twelve Realms.

1. ### **I. SLD FORM FACTORS (THE HARDWARE)**

Players must choose one of the following form factors. This is the most important decision a character makes regarding their physical safety.

1. **1\. The Spinal Graft (The "Carbon" Standard)**

The classic, permanent installation. Micro-filaments are woven into the central nervous system.

* **Convenience:** Absolute. It can never be dropped or forgotten.

* **Risk:** True Death. If your Vessel is destroyed and the graft is crushed, the soul has no "Anchor" and dissipates into the Lattice.

* **Market Value:** **2,500 CR** (Includes surgery).

* **Trait: Hard-Wired.** \+2 bonus to saves against being forcibly de-synced or hacked.

**2\. The External Module (The "Hot-Swap")**

A handheld or belt-mounted device that connects to a "Port" on the back of a Vessel’s neck via a high-tensile cable.

* **Convenience:** Moderate. You can move your soul from a combat frame to a luxury shuttle in seconds without a medical bay.

* **Risk: Theft & Severing.** The cable can be cut in combat (DV 15 to target). If the module is stolen while you are "inside" a Vessel, you are trapped in that body until the module is returned.

* **Market Value:** **5,000 CR.**

* **Trait: Rapid Transfer.** Can switch between synced devices as a Move Action instead of a 1-minute ritual.

**3\. The Internal Socket (The "Vault")**

Available only in high-end Vessels (Section 2). The SLD module is inserted into a shielded, armored internal socket deep within the Vessel’s chest or head.

* **Convenience:** High. It offers the protection of a graft with the modularity of a swap.

* **Risk: Extraction.** If an enemy paralyzes your Vessel, they can perform a "Forceful Extraction" to take your SLD (and your soul) hostage.

* **Market Value:** **10,000 CR** (Vessel must have a "Socket" slot).

* **Trait: Shielded Core.** The SLD ignores the first 10 points of damage from any source that would destroy it.

  2. ### **II. SOUL-JACKING & THE "GHOST" STATE**

Because the SLD is now a physical object that can be moved, we need rules for what happens when the connection is broken.

* **The "Husk" State:** If an SLD is removed or unplugged while the character is active, the Vessel becomes an immobile "Husk." It retains its DR/DV but can perform no actions.

* **The "Shadow" State:** The player’s consciousness remains inside the SLD module. They can hear and see through the module's sensors but cannot move.

* **Soul-Scribing:** With a **Neurathrix Data-Siphon (5,000 CR)**, an enemy can "Read" a stolen SLD to gain the character's memories, passwords, and secrets.

  3. ### **III. ADVANCED SLD UPGRADES (The "Wealth" Options)**

For those with infinite wealth, the SLD can be more than just a bridge—it can be a weapon or a fortress.

**1\. The "Ghost-Drive" Backup (Voidforge Syndicate)**

* **Effect:** A secondary, "dummy" SLD that contains a 24-hour-old snapshot of your consciousness. If the primary SLD is destroyed, the user "re-spawns" in a pre-designated clone-husk, though they lose all memories from the last 24 hours.

* **Price:** **75,000 CR** \+ **5,000 CR/month** maintenance.

**2\. The "Hard-Light" Tether (Eclipse Wardens)**

* **Effect:** Replaces the physical cable of an External Module with a beam of violet hard-light. The connection cannot be "cut" by physical blades, only by specialized Ion weapons.

* **Price:** **15,000 CR.**

**3\. The "Neural-Vermin" (Neurathrix Cartel)**

* **Effect:** If anyone other than the registered user attempts to plug in or hack the SLD, the device releases a lethal electrical discharge (3d10 Spirit Damage, DC 18 Spirit Save for half).

* **Price:** **12,000 CR.**

  4. ### **IV. MECHANICAL SIGNIFICANCE: THE "SYNC-CHECK"**

To make the SLD feel important in every session, GMs can call for a **Sync-Check (Spirit \+ SLD Quality)**:

* **When:** Entering a high-radiation zone, taking a massive hit, or attempting a complex maneuver.

* **Failure:** You suffer a "Desync Flicker." You lose your next Bonus Action as the machine's "limbs" don't respond to your brain's commands for a split second.

4. # **SECTION 1.2: BRAND SOVEREIGNTY (THE GREAT FAMILIES)**

In the Twelve Realms, technology is not a commodity; it is a lineage. While "White-Label" brands provide the functional baseline for the masses, the **Great Families** produce hardware that pushes the boundaries of the Soul Link Device.

However, corporate specialization breeds inherent flaws. No single brand can master every domain of the Soulforge. To choose a brand is to accept a specific set of tactical advantages while inheriting a calculated set of vulnerabilities.

1. ### **I. IRON VEIL COLLECTIVE (The Industrial Titans)**

“*What is Forged in Iron, Endures in Soul.”*

The Iron Veil dominates the Core-Realms' heavy industry, mining, and orbital defense. Their tech is brutalist, characterized by hammered steel, exposed hydraulics, and orange-glow thermal coils.

* **Signature Quality: Reinforced Plating.** Iron Veil gear is built to outlast the user. Their armor and vessels ignore the first 2 points of any effect that would reduce their **DR (Damage Reduction)**.

* **The Strength:** **Unmatched Durability.** They provide the highest base DR values in the game. Their weapons rarely jam, and their hulls can withstand the crushing pressures of the Deep Void.

* **The Weakness:** **System Inertia.** Iron Veil tech is heavy and power-hungry. All Iron Veil gear has its **Weight doubled** compared to White-Label equivalents, and using their "Active" abilities costs **\+2 EP (Energy Points)**.

* **Market Presence:** They are the kings of **Heavy Armor, Mining Tools, and Cargo Vessels.** They are virtually non-existent in the Stealth or Long-Range Recon markets.

II. ECLIPSE WARDENS (The High-Tier Aristocracy)

“*Precision is the Only Prayer.”*

The Wardens view technology as a religious pursuit. Their gear is "Shattered Diamond" aesthetic—white ceramics, floating shards, and violet hard-light arrays.

* **Signature Quality: Harmonic Resonance.** Eclipse Warden gear is perfectly tuned to the SLD. They gain a **\+2 bonus to Initiative** and a **\+1 bonus to all DV (Defense Value)** checks while fully synced.

* **The Strength:** **Finesse and Energy Efficiency.** Their "Hard-Light" weapons weigh nothing (**Weight: 0**) and their shields regenerate at double the standard rate.

* **The Weakness:** **Structural Fragility.** Their tech is delicate. If an Eclipse Warden item takes damage exceeding 10% of its total HP in a single hit, its special bonuses are disabled until it undergoes "Precision Calibration" in a high-tech lab.

* **Market Presence:** They dominate **Luxury Travel, High-Tier Assassination Gear, and Personal Shields.** They do not produce "Work-Horse" industrial equipment or heavy-ordnance "dirty" weapons.

  ### **III. HELIX DYNAMICS (The Bio-Engineers)**

“*Grown, Not Built.”*

Helix Dynamics has erased the line between biology and machine. Their "Vessels" are living organisms with pulsing veins and chitinous plates.

* **Signature Quality: Cellular Adaptation.** Helix gear possesses a **Regeneration** value. It heals 1 HP per minute (or 1 per combat round if "Fed"). It also grants a **\+2 bonus on Saves** against environmental hazards (radiation, toxic gas, extreme cold).

* **The Strength:** **Sustainability.** Their gear never needs "spare parts"—it just needs biomass. It is highly effective in prolonged Fringe-space operations where repair shops don't exist.

* **The Weakness:** **The Symbiotic Tax.** Their gear is "hungry." To maintain the Regeneration bonus, the user must expend **1 HP or 2 EP every 24 hours** to "feed" the item. If not fed, the item becomes *Dormant* and loses all bonuses.

* **Market Presence:** Leaders in **Bio-Husk Vessels, Medical Augments, and Chemical Weaponry.** They have zero presence in the Starship Hull or Heavy Kinetic Artillery markets.

  ### **IV. VOIDFORGE SYNDICATE (The Invisible Hand)**

“*Absent Space.”*

The Syndicate operates in the shadows of the Lattice. Their tech is matte-black, jagged, and designed to "eat" light and sensor waves.

* **Signature Quality: Ghost-Signature.** Voidforge tech is **Unscannable**. It is invisible to standard radar, thermal, and electronic sensors unless the observer is within 50 feet. Attacks made with Voidforge weapons while hidden grant **Advantage on the first hit**.

* **The Strength:** **Stealth and Gravity Control.** They manipulate local gravity fields to make heavy objects weightless or to pin enemies in place.

* **The Weakness:** **Minimal Protection.** To maintain stealth, they use thin composite lattices. Their gear offers the **lowest DR in the book** (often 0 or 1\) and is susceptible to Ion/EMP damage, which "reveals" the tech for 1d6 rounds.

* **Market Presence:** They own the **Infiltration, Stealth-Recon, and Gravity-Manipulator** markets. They have no presence in "Open Warfare" gear like tanks or brightly-lit space stations.

  ### **V. NEURATHRIX CARTEL (The Data-Slavers)**

“*Digital Obsidian.”*

The Cartel specializes in the "software" of the soul. Their gear is sleek black glass with gold circuitry traces and flickering amber holographic displays.

* **Signature Quality: Neural Siphon.** When a Neurathrix weapon deals damage, the wielder recovers **1 EP (Energy Point)**. Their gear can target an enemy's **Spirit or EP** directly instead of their physical HP.

* **The Strength:** **Electronic Warfare.** They are masters of the SLD. Their tech can "lock" an enemy's Sync-Load, forcing them to de-sync from their augments.

* **The Weakness:** **The Biological Gap.** Their tech is designed to fight *souls*. Against "Dumb" tech (automated turrets), non-sentient wildlife, or non-synced targets, their weapons deal **Half Damage**.

* **Market Presence:** They dominate **Data-Intrusion, Electronic Counter-Measures (ECM), and Soul-Siphoning weapons.** They do not produce kinetic blades or physical armor plating.

  2. ### **VI. THE SECOND TIER: UNIVERSAL & FRINGE BRANDS**

Outside of the five Great Families, the majority of the Twelve Realms relies on secondary manufacturers that prioritize cost-efficiency, modularity, or extreme-environment reliability.

1. **Apex-Industrial (The Standard):** The most common brand in the galaxy. Apex-Industrial produces everything from mess-kits to shuttle hulls.

   * **Mechanical Profile:** It has no Signature Qualities and no special weaknesses. It serves as the mechanical baseline (1.0x Cost, Standard Weight, Standard DR/DV) for all equipment in this book.

2. **Titan-Aegis (The Heavy-Duty Utility):** A brand dedicated to extreme environments and high-stress operations (Mining, Construction, Deep-Space Salvage).

   * **Mechanical Profile: Over-Engineered.** Titan-Aegis gear is immune to the "Durability Decay" rules (see Section 1.5). While it lacks the high-end performance of the Great Families, it is nearly impossible to break. It adds **\+5 to the item's HP (Hit Points)** but increases the **Sync-Load (SL) cost by \+1**.

3. **Rivet-Head (The Fringe Brand):** This is not a single company, but a classification for "Dirty" tech—gear that has been illegally modified, salvaged from battlefields, or cobbled together in the Fringe.

   * **Mechanical Profile: Unpredictable.** Every Rivet-Head item comes with **1 additional Modification Slot**, allowing for greater customization. However, any roll of a **Natural 1** while using a Rivet-Head item triggers a **Critical Malfunction** (the item ceases to function until repaired during a Short Rest).

     3. ### **SECTION 1.3: THE LATTICE & THE VOID**

In the Soulvegr universe, location dictates functionality. Technology that hums with efficiency in the Core-Realms may sputter and die in the Deep Void.

1. #### **I. The Core (The High-Lattice Zone)**

The Core is saturated with the Luminous Network.

* **The Effect:** All Soul-Linked tech operates at peak efficiency.

* **Mechanical Bonus:** Characters gain a **\+2 bonus to their EP (Energy Point) maximum** while within the Core, as the SLD draws ambient power from the surrounding Lattice.

  #### **II. The Bloom (The Unstable Zone)**

Regions where the Lattice is overgrown, wild, or fluctuating.

* **The Effect:** Technology becomes "frenzied."

* **Mechanical Penalty:** Whenever a character uses an "Active" ability on a piece of gear, they must roll a **d20**. On a **1-2**, the item draws double the required EP. On a **19-20**, the EP cost is waived as the item taps into a localized surge.

  #### **III. The Deep Void (The Zero-Lattice Zone)**

The dead space between stars, far from the light of the Soulvegr.

* **The Effect:** The "Bridge" provided by the SLD begins to fray.

* **Mechanical Penalty:** All items have their **Sync-Load (SL) cost increased by \+1**. Additionally, **Regeneration** (from Helix Dynamics or other sources) is halved, as the biological components lack the metaphysical energy required to knit cells back together.

  4. ### **SECTION 1.4: THE ECONOMY OF THE FORGE**

In the Twelve Realms, wealth is as much about access as it is about currency. The economy is a fragmented web of corporate dominance, localized barter, and the cold reality of life-support costs.

1. #### **I. Primary Currencies**

* **Standard Credits (CR):** The universal digital currency managed by the Core-Realm banks. It is accepted by all Great Families and major starports.

* **Corporate Scrip:** Issued by specific Families (e.g., Iron Veil "Voucher-Tokens"). Scrip typically grants a **10% discount** on that Family’s goods but is worthless or traded at a heavy loss elsewhere.

* **Barter & Biomass:** In the Fringe, physical goods, fuel, and raw biomass (for Helix Dynamics tech) often replace digital currency.

  #### **II. Contractual Living: "Lease-to-Own" Life Support**

For many in the deep reaches of space, survival is a subscription service.

* **The Mechanic:** Players may "Lease" high-tier gear (Vessels or Ships) for **10% of its total cost per month**.

* **The Catch:** Failure to pay the lease results in a **Remote Lockout**. The item’s SLD connection is severed, rendering it useless until the debt is settled.

  5. ### **SECTION 1.5: MAINTENANCE & DEGENERATION**

Technology in the Soulvegr universe is under constant assault from radiation, micro-meteors, and the strain of the SLD.

1. #### **I. Durability Decay**

Every piece of gear has a **Durability Rating** (Standard is 5).

* **The Trigger:** Whenever a character rolls a **Natural 1** on an attack or check using a piece of gear, or when they take a **Critical Hit** while synced to a Vessel, the item's Durability drops by 1\.

* **The Penalty:** When Durability reaches 0, the item becomes **Damaged**.

  * **Weapons:** \-2 to hit and damage.

  * **Armor/Vessels:** DR is halved (minimum 1).

  * **Electronics:** SL cost increases by \+2.

  #### **II. Part Scavenging & Repairs**

* **Field Repair:** A Mind \+ Technology check (DC 15\) and 1 hour can restore 1 point of Durability if the player has a **Repair Kit**.

* **Scavenging:** Players can strip "Dirty" tech from defeated enemies. Roll Mind \+ Technology; a success provides **Scrap Components** equal to the item's Weight. Scrap can be used to bypass the Credit cost of repairs in the Fringe.
